Room: 11.0 × 8.4 × 4.15 m. Units metres.

## Zones
- **Stacks** — built-in wooden bays on the back wall (8) and left wall (3+1), packed books.
- **Reading hall** — two long tables on a runner; chairs face the tables.
- **Catalog / staff** — one study desk, lectern, card catalog, returns cart.
- **Entrance** — open cutaway corner with a new-arrivals case and plant.

## Circulation
Stack aisle behind the rear table, centre runner between tables, path from the open corner to the catalog.
