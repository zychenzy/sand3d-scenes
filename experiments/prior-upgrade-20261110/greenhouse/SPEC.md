# Indoor greenhouse — even-span glasshouse (prior upgrade)

A working ridge-and-gutter glasshouse, not a ring of pots on an empty floor.

## Composition

Room 8.8 m × 7.2 m. Even-span roof: eaves at 2.42 m, ridge at 3.62 m. Back and left sides are glazed above a brick knee. The front and the right side are the cutaway: rafters and a right-hand colonnade stay, the glass does not.

Circulation is a gravel spine just right of centre, from the open front to the potting corner, with a cross-path to the sitting nook. An island bed sits left of the spine. Staging shelves sit on the back wall.

### Raised beds

- Back bed: a multi-stem shrub, ferns, a big-leaf, a flower spike, and a climber on a trellis against the gable.
- Left bed: a palm, a low big-leaf plant, ferns, and a climber on the mullions.
- Island bed (focal): a branching canopy tree, a lower multi-stem shrub, ferns, and blossom spikes.

### Staging

Stepped wooden greenhouse benches on the back wall. Seed trays on the lowest board, terracotta pots on the upper two.

### Potting bench

Back-right, surface at 0.93 m. Plank top, legs, slat shelf, three drawers, splash, soil tray, trowel, seed packets. A galvanized can and a hose sit on the floor. Catalog pieces on and above the bench: two differently scaled pots with cuttings, a brush pot, jars, a mug, a banker’s lamp, a wall shelf with a thermos, and a round stool in front of the bench. A larger pot with a fern stands off the path.

### Sitting nook and path bench

Front-left. A backless museum bench with a cushion faces a round tea table. The dining chair is on the path side of the table. Its model faces local +Z; `rotationY` of −π/2 turns that face toward −X, into the table and the bench. A second museum bench on the spine faces the island bed (−X).

## Light

Warm key from the open front-right casts hard shadows through the rafters and the canopy. A leaf-cookie spotlight mottles the island and the path. Pendants over the nook and the path, plus the bench lamp, are small warm pools. Unlit mode keeps the same colors without those lights.
