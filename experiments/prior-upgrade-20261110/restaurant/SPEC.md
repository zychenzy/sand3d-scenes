# Hinoki dining — brief

A Japanese dining room, richer than the v1 `restaurant/` box of a booth island plus two 6-tops.

Architecture: cutaway plaster room with hinoki wainscot, chair rail, cornice, left-wall slats, a mullioned back window with an exterior, a noren brand band, and a coffered partial ceiling with a warm cove. No shoji, screens, or partitions.

Zones: wall booths (three complete diner pairs against the left wall), table dining (three enlarged 8-tops, eight matching chairs facing inward), sushi pass (wood counter, plate stacks, soy, vertical menu). Circulation is the red runner between booths and tables.

Light: cool window directional, warm key from the cutaway, point pools at lanterns / pendants / pass strip. Contact blobs under furniture. Emissive lantern paper and pendant shades in toon and unlit.

Room: 11.6 × 8.8 × 3.28 m. Units metres. Cutaway opens toward +X and +Z.
