# Rehearsal hall — prior upgrade

A single cutaway room, not a furniture grid. The back wall is the practice dais. Listening chairs face that wall across a centre aisle. Acoustic treatment is part of the architecture.

## Zones

- Practice dais, back wall. Shallow riser, nosing, a step, and a cable raceway. Upright piano (procedural) with catalog bench on the keyboard side. Drum kit stage left. Guitar combo + Marshall + a small mixer stage right.
- PA. Two catalog speakers on tripod stands, toed into the floor, drivers toward the practice area.
- Listening / coaching. Four dining chairs in two pairs facing the dais, centre aisle kept clear. A coach chair and a small table with lamp and mug sit to the right, turned toward the piano.
- Score rack, left wall. Catalog book stand and wall shelf with procedural folders. Studio door with a vision panel toward the front-left corner.
- Instrument stand. Acoustic guitar on a chrome A-frame, left of the dais. Two music stands with sheet music at the dais lip, outside the aisle.

## Acoustic treatment

- Wedge foam grid on the practice wall, two tones, some cells skipped.
- Corner bass traps.
- Quadratic wood slat diffuser on the left wall.
- Three hung clouds on rods under the partial ceiling.
- Clerestory over the foam, emissive in toon and unlit.

## Scale

Room 10.6 × 8.2 × 3.45 m. Dais, chair facing, stand heights, and aisle are in metres in `layout.json`.

## Exclusions

No U-sofa, no coffee table as the focus, no karaoke screen. Must read as a rehearsal room, not a KTV suite.
