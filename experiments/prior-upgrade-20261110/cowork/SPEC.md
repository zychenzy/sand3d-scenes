# Shared study / cowork — brief (prior-upgrade)

Redo of pre-sprint `cowork/` to match sprint-20261110 authored density. Distinct from the library: no stacks.

Architecture: locker bays of unequal width on the left wall, chalkboard niche plus three window sashes on the back wall, acoustic baffles and a cable tray on a partial ceiling, planter partition, layered plaster/oak trim.

Furniture: eight study desks in four clusters (window, focus, workshop, island), a square huddle table, a coffee-table lounge, monitors facing sitters, banker lamps on tops, pendants, lockers, shelves, plants.

Light: cool window directional and spills; warm points at lamps, pendants, and the board; hard shadows; contact blobs; emissive shades, bulbs, and monitor glow.

Room: 12.2 × 8.8 × 3.6 m. Units metres. Cutaway opens toward +X and +Z.
