Metres. Toon hard, 4-step gradient, one hard shadow from the key light plus practical point and spot lights.

Cutaway room 10.4 × 8.4 × 3.14. Back wall is -Z, left wall is -X. Front and right stay open. A short right stub and a plaster pier form the kiln alcove.

`rotationY` 0 looks toward +Z. Forward is `(sin(rotationY), cos(rotationY))`.

- **Drying** — rack centred at (0.48, -3.88). Shelf boards are unequal and not fully loaded.
