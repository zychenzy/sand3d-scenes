# GAP — prior-upgrade gym

Catalog still has no squat racks, dumbbells, kettlebells, bumper plates, mats, battle ropes, plyo boxes, lockers, or cardio machines. Those are procedural at human scale in `gym.js`.

Closed in this redo:

- Equipment zones plus a clear turf lane instead of three bags on a red carpet.
- Full-height squat rack, wall-scale dumbbell run, plate tree, kettlebells.
- Body-length stretch mats facing the mirror, with walking gaps.
- Rest benches on the open edge facing the training floor.
- Layered lighting: clerestory, high-bays, warm pendants, hemi/key/fill/points.
- Contact decals under bags, rack, benches, and floor props.
- Richer cutaway shell: rubber tiles, oxblood bag field, wainscot, I-beams, duct, mirror, WOD board.

Remaining:

- No treadmills / bikes / rowers — room reads as bag + iron + stretch, not a full commercial cardio floor.
- Museum benches are still the crossed-steel catalog bake, tinted cooler.
- Heavy bags stay the stand-mounted catalog bake (not ceiling-hung).
- Mirror is a toon glass pane, not a real reflection.
