# Bag-and-iron training gym — brief

Redo of the pre-sprint bag room as a zoned cutaway: striking, free weights, open turf, stretch mats, rest.

## Zones

| zone | contents |
|------|----------|
| bags | Three stand-mounted heavy bags, 2.4 m centres, 1.55 m striking pads, oxblood rubber, wall mirror behind |
| strength | 2.32 m squat rack facing the floor, 2.2 m dumbbell run, plate tree, kettlebells |
| floor | Turf lane with battle ropes, plyo boxes, medicine balls — circulation, not clutter |
| mats | Three 0.62 × 1.82 m mats facing the mirror, ≥ 0.55 m walking gaps |
| rest | Two benches on the open edge face −Z (training floor); lockers + water stoop at left-front |

## Scale

- Heavy bag stand 2.15 m (catalog bake)
- Squat rack uprights 2.32 m, 1.22 × 1.48 m plan
- Yoga/stretch mat 0.62 × 1.82 m
- Bumper plates Ø 0.45 m
- Kettlebells ~0.28 m tall
- Locker bank 1.86 m
