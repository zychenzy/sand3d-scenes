# Small cinema screening room — prior-upgrade

A small repertory house, not a multiplex and not the boutique screening-room from the same sprint.

Focal point: one large screen on the −Z wall, brass reveal, tied burgundy drapes, a pelmet, and a sunset picture on the screen (not a blank panel). Speakers sit on plinths just outside the drapes.

House: three ranked rows of the same theatre chair, split left/right of a **clear center aisle**. Side aisles as well. Each landing has stair treads, brass nosings, and puck lights. Front row on a low dais; middle and rear rise in a rake. Outer seats toe in toward the screen. Native axis is −Z at `rotationY` 0.

Second zone: a rear foyer at the top landing — concession/ticket counter, popcorn kettle, drinks, coats, illuminated EXIT, hanging projector aimed at the screen. You enter at the back and walk down.

Excluded: dining tables, sofas, mixed seat types, a uniform 5×3 block with no aisle.

Room envelope about 10.4 × 8.9 × 3.52 m, open on +X past the screen return and on +Z.
