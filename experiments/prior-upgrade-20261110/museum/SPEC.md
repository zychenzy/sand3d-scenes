# North Hall gallery — brief

A pale stone museum gallery, richer than the v1 `museum/` box of solid-column cases and two floating benches.

Architecture: stone tile floor with grout and a cutaway curb; plaster walls with skirting, dado, chair rail, picture rail, and cornice; coffered partial ceiling with a mullioned skylight well; left-wall niche and a doorway into the next hall.

Furniture / display: two long slab benches facing the picture wall; procedural limestone plinths with figure / torso / slabs / bust; glass standing and table vitrines with objects inside (not solid wood columns); visitor lectern. No sofas.

Light: cool skylight directional plus a skylight point; warm key from the cutaway; track and picture-light points; pendant points over sculptures; hard shadows; contact blobs under furniture.

Room: 13.8 × 9.6 × 4.75 m. Units metres. Cutaway opens toward +X and +Z.
