# Gaps

The catalog has no yoga mat, bolster, block, curtain, cubby, or clerestory asset. Those are procedural meshes in `stretch.js`: thickened mats with a head band, cylinders for rolls and the bolster, folded boxes for blankets, open timber cubbies, and a framed mirror with a five-pane clerestory.

Other limits:

- The benches are museum benches (oak seat, crossed steel legs), tinted warmer. They are backless, so either side is a seat. Long axis is Z; they sit toward the practice floor.
- Pots are the cafe flowerpot. Leaves are a few boxes and cylinders, not a scanned plant.
- Pendant emissive bulbs are extra spheres. The GLB shade itself is not an emitter.
- Contact grounding is a radial decal plus a hard shadow map, not screen-space AO.
- The toon gradient's darkest stop is lifted (about 0.34) so shadowed timber does not crush to black.
- Mirror glass is a pale toon pane. It does not reflect the room.
- Clerestory glass is a transparent toon pane. It does not refract.
- No people, and no cloth simulation. Drapes are overlapping slabs at the mirror jambs.
- Cubbies are open timber bays facing the practice floor. They are not scanned locker carcasses.
