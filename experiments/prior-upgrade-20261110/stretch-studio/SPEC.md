# Clerestory stretch studio

A 9.4 × 7.8 × 3.22 m cutaway. Front (+Z) and right (+X) are open. Back and left are sage plaster with a timber rail, sage base, and vertical oak slats.

## Zones

**Practice floor.** A dark timber border sets the mat area. The instructor mat sits on a 90 mm platform at the studio mirror, head band toward the class. Five student mats sit in two rows with at least 0.55 m of clear edge gap. Students face the mirror. Blocks, a folded blanket, and one bolster are the only mat props.

**Cubby and entry.** Open timber cubbies on the left wall hold rolled mats, folded textiles, blocks, and a bolster. A hook rail hangs straps. Shoe cubbies and a rest bench sit toward the open front. A second rest bench sits on the open right, long axis along Z, sitting toward the practice floor. Water (thermos + mugs) sits on the cubby top.

## Architecture

The focal wall is a full-width studio mirror with timber stiles and a five-pane clerestory above it. Linen drapes gather at both jambs. A low prop ledge under the mirror holds cork blocks, a bronze bowl, and a small pot. Exposed beams and a cove strip sit on the partial ceiling.

## Shading

Local `scene_lib.js` keeps the four-step toon gradient, with the shadow stop lifted so timber color stays visible. Hard shadow map. Clerestory key from the back, warm fill from the open front, three pendant bulbs and the ledge candle are emissive. Mats, cubbies, benches, and pots get contact pads.

## Palette

Sage plaster, pale oak, linen, terracotta, sand, mauve, slate blue. Calm north-ish light, not a cafe amber wash.
