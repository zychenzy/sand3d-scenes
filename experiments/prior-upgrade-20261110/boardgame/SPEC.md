# The Night Board

A board-game club room, not a second copy of `boardgame/` (three empty square tables) or `cafe/` (round coffee tables and a service bar).

Zones:

- **Play floor.** One 1.88 × 0.98 m trestle table (hex campaign, six chairs), one 1.16 m square (checkers with men on the squares, four chairs), one 1.14 m square (cards on green felt, four chairs), one 1.02 m go board (two chairs facing each other). Rugs mark each group. A centre aisle stays open between the back pair and the front pair. Chairs use the dining-chair GLB, whose backrest is local −Z, and face the tabletop.
- **Box wall.** Display-case GLBs along the back and left walls, filled with face-out game boxes. Wall shelves on the right of the back wall hold more boxes. A low feature table and two return crates sit on the floor.
- **Night-board nook.** Painted club-night schedule on the left wall, a lectern with a rulebook, coat pegs by the open front.

Pendants hang over every play table and along the box wall. Spot lights make a warm pool on each surface. The key light is kept modest so those pools cross a toon band.
