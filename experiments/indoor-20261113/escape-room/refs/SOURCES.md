# Sources

Links only. No reference photos, candidate renders, or virtualenvs are stored in git.

Composition, not a traced room:

- Harbor and signal interiors — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Interiors_of_lighthouses
- Combination cabinets and escape-room locks — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Escape_rooms
- Banker’s lamp on a writing desk — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Bankers_lamps
- Brass engine-room telegraphs, for the wheel panel — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Chadburns

The room is a daylight cipher study: chart, slate, wheels, and three colored cabinets. It is not a copy of any one lighthouse or escape room.

Asset geometry is the existing sand3d bakes listed in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. Each GLB was opened in a toon render (front, side) before it was placed. Notes from that pass are in `GAP.md`.
