# Tide Lock — brief

Harbor cipher study, cutaway on +X and +Z. Back wall −Z, window wall −X.

The puzzle is one combination told four ways: the easel chart, the keeper’s slate, the jar tags on the clue table, and the wheels on the mechanical panel. A tide staff on the window wall and the chart chest repeat the same three marks. Three cabinets are built into one oak bank. The lantern door is the one left open, with the key inside.

Walk the runner and brass strip from the floor rose, past the investigation desk, to the panel bench. A side branch reaches the window desk. Chairs face the desks and the clue table. The lamp shade points toward the sitter. The easel canvas and the slate face the room.
