# Tide Lock — brief

Harbor cipher study. Back wall −Z, window wall −X, right wall closed. The front (+Z) is the viewing opening.

The puzzle is one combination told four ways: the easel chart, the keeper’s slate, the jar tags on the clue table, and the wheels on the mechanical panel. A tide staff on the window wall and the chart chest repeat the same three marks. Three cabinets are built into one oak bank. The lantern door is the one left open, with the key inside.

Walk the runner and brass strip from the floor rose, past the investigation desk, to the panel bench. A side branch reaches the window desk. Chairs face the desks and the clue table. The lamp shade points toward the sitter. The easel canvas faces the room. The keeper’s copy is a slate hung on the back wall. The floor is a quiet oak board pattern. A plaster ceiling with hung beams closes the roof. The right wall carries the same beadboard, chair rail, and picture rail as the back wall, so the frames do not open onto empty sky.

See `layout.json` for poses, cameras, and the circulation polyline. `tests/check_layout.py` checks them.
