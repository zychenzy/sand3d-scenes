# Sources

Links only. Reference photos are not stored in git.

Real shops and workshops this room is drawn from:

- [Den Gamle By — watchmaker’s shop](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Den_Gamle_By_-_Urmager_02.jpg) — Leif Jørgensen, 2023. A single historic shop room: bench against the wall, clocks, and a customer side. CC BY-SA.
- [Den Gamle By — Urmager](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Den_gamle_By_-_Urmager.jpg) — the same Aarhus museum shop, wider view.
- [Maihaugen watchmaker’s workshop](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maihaugen,_Urmakerverkstedet_-_no-nb_digifoto_20150219_00102_NB_MIT_FNR_19111.jpg) — Norwegian museum workshop, bench and stored movements.
- [Davis Brothers Clock Shop interior](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Davis_Brothers_Clock_Shop_interior,_1840_Park_Avenue.jpg) — a clock-shop wall of cases, not a jewelry salon.
- [18th-century Geneva watchmaker’s atelier](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atelier_d%E2%80%99horloger_au_XVIIIe_si%C3%A8cle_%C3%A0_Gen%C3%A8ve.jpg) — raised bench, window light, and a wall of work.
- [Watchmaker’s tools, Handwerksmuseum Augsburg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Handwerksmuseum_Augsburg_10.jpg) — loupe, turns, and small steel tools.
- [Roscheider Hof watchmaker’s lathe](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roscheiderhof-lg1-uhrmacher-2a.jpg) — the lathe is noted as a gap; the bench context is the reference.
- [Horlogerie du Passage, Paris](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/10/20/fashion/watches-repair-shop-paris.html) — one room, tables facing the window, a wall of tiny parts drawers (New York Times, 2022).

Catalog credits for the reused GLBs are in `../ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. The programs come from [Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse).
