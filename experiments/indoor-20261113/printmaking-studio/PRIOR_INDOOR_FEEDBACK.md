# Prior indoor feedback

Historical notes are the brief for this scene. They are not scores, and this folder does not rebuild an older room.

## What was already said

- Earlier indoor scenes were too crude, too sparse, and too monotonous.
- Shading and rendering were too simple.
- Adding more objects under the same shader does not fix that. The room needs a clearer design.
- Check orientation, table and tool proportions, and walkways.
- Unlit frames are part of the review, so the geometry has to read without lighting.

## What I looked at

I opened the prior-upgrade Wheelhouse pottery stills (`experiments/prior-upgrade-20261110/workshop/shots/toon_default.png`, `toon_close.png`, `unlit_default.png`). That is the nearest finished craft-shop redo. I did not assign it a score, and I did not restage it.

What is visible there:

- The room is already zoned: wheels along one wall, a kiln with a mouth that stays orange in unlit, a center table, a rack, windows, and a brick dado. It is not an empty box.
- Toon and unlit show the same silhouettes. The toon pass mainly adds shadow bands. Surfaces are flat poster colors: no grain, no stain, no image on the work.
- A lot of the furniture is one wood brown, so the island, chairs, rack, and bench blend together until the kiln and the brick break the palette.
- The close frame makes the wheel readable, and the wheel is still a simple lathed solid.

## What this shop does with that

- The plan is an intaglio shop, not another wood-toned studio. Clean paper is west. The press is center. Ink, the hot plate, ferric, and the ink sink are east.
- The hero silhouette is a bed, roller, cheeks, and a star wheel, with felts and a plate on the bed. There is no easel.
- Work tops are shop-sized: inking 2.40 × 1.16 at 0.92 m, paper 2.20 × 1.10 at 0.78 m. Chairs and the stool face those tops. The bay in front of the wheel is kept clear.
- Materials are split on purpose: oak and iron on the press, maple on the paper table, black glass on the slab, steel flat files, stainless sinks, teal aprons, copper plates, concrete with local stains.
- Sheets, plates, and the inking glass carry drawn maps, so unlit still shows an image and a plate mark, not only a tinted box.
- The gradient is a hard uneven 4-step map, with one key shadow, warm practicals on the work, and a cool tube in the hood.
