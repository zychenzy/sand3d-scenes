# Gaps

The catalog pendant is a domestic cone used across the cafe and pottery rooms. It is not in this shop. The wall shelf is a generic four-tier bake. It sits in the clean corner for paper samples. Its exact shelf heights are the baked mesh, not measured millwork.

Windows are glazed panels on the back wall, not cut openings. The floor is instanced concrete slabs with ink, rust, water, and ferric stains, not a scanned floor.
