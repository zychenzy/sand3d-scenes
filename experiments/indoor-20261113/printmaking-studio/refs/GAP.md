# Gaps

Catalog meshes are used in place, by relative path. This folder has no new GLB.

Nothing in the shared catalog is an etching press, brayer, drying rack, flat file, guillotine, ferric tray, or copper plate. Those are built in `print.js`.

Compromises:

- The inking and paper stations keep the dining-table GLBs as layout anchors, but those meshes are hidden. The visible benches are procedural: thick maple tops, aprons, square legs, and a lower shelf. The glass slab still sits on the inking top.
- The cafe stool and the dining chair are hidden the same way. The seats you see are shop stools: a round wood seat, four straight legs, and a foot rail.
- A paper cart with a BFK stack sits in the left foreground, outside the entry aisle. The aisle itself stays empty. A closer default camera drops the drying racks out of the frame.
- Ink tins are cafe jam jars with the glass recolored. Lids stay the baked color. One procedural tin is the open black.
- Portfolios live in the library display bookcase. Shelves read as flat stacks, not a map cabinet. The real flat file is procedural and only five drawers deep.
- The edition log is the cowork chalkboard, with a chalk texture parented to the writing face. It is a school board, not a shop slate.
- The sorting seat is the museum bench, turned parallel to the front drying rack. It has no back and no casters.
- Wall-shelf end panels in that bake are skewed, so they are hidden and replaced with side cheeks.
- The banker’s lamp is aimed across the slab from the back-left wood margin. Its shade is wide for the border it sits on.
- Hanging prints are canvas drawings with a plate mark and an edition number, not scans. One sheet on the press bed is the same kind of drawing, laid flat.
- The press wheel is a torus, six spokes, and a brass grip. It is not a scanned star wheel. Blankets are three boxes plus one hanging corner.
- Glass, the soaking tray, and the window panes are opaque or faintly unlit. Real transparency sorts badly under the toon path.
- The north light is a clerestory in the left wall plus a flat sky card outside it. There is no modeled street.
- Shadows are PCF soft. The toon ramp is still four hard steps. A hard shadow map turned the wheel spokes into noise on the bed.
- The ceiling is a full lid, and the key light enters through the clerestory instead of over the roof. Beams, pipes, and the ceiling slab do not cast. When the slab cast, it blacked out the north wall. When the beams cast, they painted four diagonal stripes on that wall. Window mullions, the press, and the furniture still cast.
- The press cheeks are an extruded casting with a bearing hole, not four posts. The wheel is still a torus with six spokes and a brass grip. The bed is still a plate.
- Floor boards are a narrow value range so the press and paper stay in front of the grain. The seams are still there.
- A real shop often puts ferric chloride in a separate exhausted room. Here it is a lidded cart on the east side, with the wash alcove, and the paper is on the back wall. The separation is distance, not a door.
- No printer figure, no moving bed, no running water.
