# SOURCES — Copperplate Atelier

No reference photographs are stored in git.

The plan follows a small professional intaglio shop, not a painting classroom:

- Charles Brand / Takach-style etching press: long bed, top roller between cheeks, star wheel on the near side, felts over the plate.
- Inking is standing, on a dark slab, with brayers, cans, and tarlatan. Paper tearing is seated, at about 0.78 m, on a top large enough for a full sheet plus a stack.
- Flat files hold paper. A soak sink is on the clean side. Ferric, the hot plate, and the ink sink are on the other side, with a vent over the tray.
- Prints dry on lines under the windows and in a slot rack that is not fully loaded.

Asset programs already baked in this repo: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse

Per-object credits are in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. This folder does not vendor a second copy of the dataset.
