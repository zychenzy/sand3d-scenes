Links only. No reference photos are stored in git.

The plan follows a single-printer intaglio shop: press as the nucleus, inking kept off the paper, drying within reach of the bed, flat files for sheets, and a separate wash for plates and solvent.

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intaglio_(printmaking)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etching
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brayer_(printing)
- https://penland.org/studios/printmaking/
- https://printshop.org/studio
- https://angelsgateart.org/arts-education/community-classes/community-class-facilities/printmaking-studio-equipment-list/
- https://nontoxic-print.com/intaglio-manual-the-acrylic-resist-etching-workshop/
- https://www.etchinginsights.com/my-etching-studio-2/

Penland’s notes (presses, inking slabs, soaking sink, drying racks, flat files) and the Lower East Side Printshop equipment list fixed the station list. Kevin Cummins’s studio note is why the paper table, soaking tray, and flat files are a clean zone with walking room, not stacked against the press. The nontoxic intaglio manual is why the inking slab is near the press but not in the paper bay, and why the ferric tub is lidded and away from the sheets.

Models are existing bakes from Hugging Face `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse` (CC-BY-4.0), referenced in place. Per-object credits are in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
