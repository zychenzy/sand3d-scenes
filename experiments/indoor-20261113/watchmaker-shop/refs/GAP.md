# Gaps

- `sand3d` and `bpy` are not installed here, and `sand3d` is not on PyPI. No new program was baked. Useful roles that already had GLBs (stools, chairs, lamps, register, jars, shelf, bookcase, table, bench, pedestal) are symlinked. Everything watch-specific is procedural.
- Wristwatches are about 10 cm across and pocket watches larger, so the dials survive the toon room. Real pieces are ~40 mm and would disappear.
- The museum vitrine is a solid walnut pedestal. Watches sit on the cap. It is not a glazed case. The intake counter is the glass case.
- Loupe, bench pin, staking lathe, ultrasonic tank, and timing machine are stylized solids, not scanned tools. The timing screen is a canvas, not a real trace.
- The wall shelf and the tall right-hand cabinet sit on the back-right and are easy to crop in the default frame. Jars on that shelf are in the layout; they are not the hero of the wide shot.
- Drawer labels read at the close and corner cameras. Across the whole room they shrink to small plates.
- Contact shadows are discs. The vendored toon path has no ambient occlusion.
- The left bench stool is behind the counter from the default camera. The close shot shows the seat at the bench.
