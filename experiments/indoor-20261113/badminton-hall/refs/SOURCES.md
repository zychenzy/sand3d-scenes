## Laws and court geometry

BWF Laws of Badminton, court and equipment (40 mm lines, 13.40 × 6.10 doubles, 5.18 singles, posts 1.55 m on the doubles sidelines, net 760 mm deep, top tape 75 mm, 1.524 m at centre and 1.55 m over the sidelines). Diagram A notes the diagonal as 14.723 m and that the same court is used for singles and doubles:

## Hall envelope, light, and background

BWF specifications for international-standard facilities. Other sanctioned tournaments: 9 m clear of girders over the court, at least 2 m behind each baseline and 1.5 m from each sideline, including between side-by-side courts. Grade 1 asks for 12 m and recommends 4 m between courts when officials and boards are present. This hall uses the 9 m / 2 m / 2 m club-international envelope, not the 12 m show-court envelope:

https://system.bwfbadminton.com/documents/folder_1_81/Statutes/CHAPTER-5---TECHNICAL-REGULATIONS/Section%205.3.4%20-%20Specs%20for%20Int'l%20Standard%20Facilities.pdf

Badminton England / Sport England design guidance (December 2011), still the cited hall note: run-off table, 9 m preferred clear height, parallel timber strips rather than herringbone, medium-dark green or blue walls at about 30–50% reflectance, no daylight, and luminaires over the run-offs at least 750 mm clear of the sidelines rather than directly over the court. The figure caption also says the net line is not marked on the floor, and dimensions are to the outside of the lines:

https://www.worldbadminton.com/reference/documents/badminton-design-guide-dec-2011.pdf

sportscotland court sheet, same 13.40 × 6.10 court, 2 m behind the baseline and 1.5 m at the side:

https://sportscotland.org.uk/media/ucxd30mw/021_-_badminton_court.pdf
