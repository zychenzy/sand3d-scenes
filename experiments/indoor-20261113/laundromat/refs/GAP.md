# Gaps

What a real neighbourhood laundry has that this room only approximates.

- Washers stay the white front-loader mesh, with a black gasket, a dark drum, and a soap drawer on the fascia. Dryers are the same family of round-door cabinet, repainted slate, with a pale door ring, a warm drum, a lint hatch, and a top vent. The difference is visible without a brand mark. They are still not a commercial stacked pair, and the doors do not open.
- The combo is a single domestic cabinet with the door on local +X, scaled to about 1.02 m. It is labeled 12kg. It does not have a separate dryer drum on top.
- The vendor is a generic glass-front machine signed 洗涤剂 PODS. It is not a soap-specific dispenser. Branded drink and detergent machines were rejected.
- No laundry cart turned up in the useful Objaverse hits (golf and mine carts did). The three wire carts are procedural.
- The clothes airer is SergeyB's short "5 Clothes Dryer", scaled up from a native height near 0.50 m to 0.78 m. It is a drying rack, not a tumble dryer.
- Detergent bottles are a thin 1220 ml bottle. They read on the shelf and disappear at the establishing distance.
- Folded garments are rounded slabs with a drooping lip and a crease. They read as towels rather than books. They are still procedural, not simulated cloth, and the lip is a simple tilted slab.
- The bench is the backless museum bench, seat height 0.46 m, used as extra waiting. It is not a laundromat banquette.
- Machine doors do not open. Cycle lights are emissive plates, not animated.
- The establishing camera includes the change machine, the price board, the waiting row, and both banks. The pod vendor sits at the left edge of that frame; the corner camera shows it in full. The price board is readable straight on and foreshortened in the wide shot.
- Signs are canvas text (WenQuanYi Micro Hei / Droid Sans Fallback at capture time), not modeled lettering.
- The ceiling is a partial slab so the cutaway stays open. Its underside uses a small emissive lift; a downward face under the hard gradient otherwise goes black.
- Domestic cabinets were scaled to about 1.02 m (washers) and 1.08 m (dryers). A full commercial front-loader is often taller.
