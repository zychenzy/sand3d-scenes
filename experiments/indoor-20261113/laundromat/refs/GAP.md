# Gap

The catalog has no washer, dryer, laundry cart, or detergent jug. Those pieces are original geometry in `laundromat.js`. Existing GLBs are referenced, not copied. See `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.

## Machines

Standard washers follow the Alliance SFNN envelope: 0.68 m wide, 0.704 m deep, 1.114 m high. White enamel, navy cap and toe, soap slots, one round door.

Each dryer column uses the AM18-0055 stack envelope (0.683 m wide, about 0.71 m deep, 1.943 m high) as a **two-dryer** cabinet: two round doors, a lint slot, and a plain steel panel. AM18-0055 itself is a washer-over-dryer combo. This scene does not copy that product. Depth is 0.71 m against the sheet’s 0.704 m. The cabinet steel is a low-contrast mottled color. A high-contrast vertical brush map was turning into bands under the 4-step toon gradient, so that map is gone.

The two larger washers (0.86 × 0.78 × 1.28 m) are a neighbourhood “big machine” scale. They are not taken from a cited sheet.

Washers and dryers are different cabinets. A washer is white enamel with a navy cap, soap slots, and one door into a smooth dark drum with three short wall lifters. A dryer is taller steel with two doors, a perforated drum back, and a warm drum on the running pockets. The drum is a recessed open cylinder set behind dark glass and a metal ring. The glass and ring swing on a hinge. From the head-on close camera a fully swung door is an edge-on wire, so the open dryer (column index 3, lower pocket) stays ajar. The open washer (standard index 3) swings into the aisle, short of the 1.48 m clearance.

Folded textiles are a short pile plus a rolled hem, not stacked slabs with stripe maps. Overhead, a DRYERS panel and a WASHERS panel replace the blank teal soffit bars that the old default camera cropped into green strips. The key shadow is a 4096 hard map on a tighter frustum, and the thin table lips do not cast.

## Detergent

There is no jug GLB. The vendor’s colored bottles are procedural and are the detergent read. Jam jars on the attendant shelf are the small-bottle stand-in, credited as jam jars. The straw-holder crock and the tray caddy sit on the folding counter as a crock and a caddy, not as detergent.

## Seating

Four metal chairs and one backless bench. Coin-op planning often asks for about one seat per 100 sq ft. This room is about 104 m², so that rule would want more seats. The cutaway keeps the street corner open, so seating is the window group plus two chairs at the fold table. Chairs face the machines (rotation near π, facing −Z). The bench’s long axis runs along X under the storefront window.

## What the committed cameras omit

The bench and the storefront glass are in the model. The four committed frames prioritize the two machine banks, the folding aisle, and the service pier, so the bench is mostly outside those frames. Orbit from the default camera reaches it.
