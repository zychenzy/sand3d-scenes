# SOURCES — Northlane

Links only. No reference photographs are stored in this scene.

Lane, approach, gutter, foul-line, and pin-spot dimensions follow the USBC Equipment Specifications and Certifications Manual:

https://bowl.com/getmedia/08ef148d-c0e4-4e00-9e0d-855ba4729ad5/equipment-specs-manual.pdf

Approach guide stations (maximum of seven guides at 2–6 in, 3–4 ft, 6–7 ft, 9–10 ft, 11–12 ft, and 14–15 ft from the foul line) are in that manual. The seven arrows at 15 ft are the common embedded range-finder layout, cross-checked against:

https://www.bowlingball.com/BowlVersity/bowling-lane-specifications

https://www.flybowling.com/bowling-alley-lane-dimensions-standards-installation-guide.html

Pair arrangement — two lanes, a shared ball return, settees behind a clear approach, masking and a dark pit — is the ordinary certified house, as in a natural pair (USBC) and in houses such as:

https://www.thegutterbowling.com/

https://brooklynbowl.com/
