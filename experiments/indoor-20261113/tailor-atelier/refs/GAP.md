- Shears are two blades and two loops. Bolts are textured cylinders with cardboard ends.
- The reused dining chair has a ladder back. From the south cameras the sewing chairs read as chairs facing the machines. From directly behind, the same back can read as a small ladder. A navy jacket hangs on the client chair for that reason.
- The flowerpot in the pressing corner reads as a small pot or bucket at the locked corner camera.
- The thread frame and notions dresser sit on the open east wall. They are in the scene and in the layout test; the four locked cameras only catch their edge.
- One hard key from the window side, plus a warm fill and two pendants. Contact discs stand in for AO. Window panes are tinted basic-material cards.
- Cloth and the pattern are canvas textures, not scanned weaves.
