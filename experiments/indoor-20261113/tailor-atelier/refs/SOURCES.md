## Room

These public pages describe the stations this room is built around. The scene is not a copy of any one photograph.

- [NK French dressmaking atelier, 1933](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interi%C3%B6r_fr%C3%A5n_NKs_Franska_damskr%C3%A4dderi_NMA.0099425.jpg) — Nordiska museet. Long work tables, a sewing machine in the foreground, a garment on a dress form beside the fitting talk.
- [Henry Poole workroom, Savile Row, 1944](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Savile_Row-_Tailoring_at_Henry_Poole_and_Co.,_London,_England,_UK,_1944_D21864.jpg) — Imperial War Museums / Ministry of Information. A dense jacket workroom. Those tailors sit on the benches; this scene uses seated machine tables and a standing cutting height instead.
- [Category: Tailors workshops](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Tailors_workshops) — including [Den Gamle By men's tailor shop](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Den_Gamle_By_-_Herreskr%C3%A6dderi_01.jpg).
- [Category: Dress forms](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Dress_forms) — toile and taped torso, which the two forms are aiming at.

Standing cutting height is 0.92 m. Seated sewing height is 0.76 m. The east aisle between the cutting table and the sewing chairs is about 1.35 m. The chair front sits about 0.38 m clear of the sewing-table edge.
