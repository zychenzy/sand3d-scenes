# SOURCES — bespoke tailor atelier

Links only. Reference photographs are not stored in git.

Spatial cues for a workroom where tailors sit to coats, a separate mirror fitting, a raised client platform, pattern cutting, and cloth bunches:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Savile_Row-_Tailoring_at_Henry_Poole_and_Co.,_London,_England,_UK,_1944_D21864.jpg — Henry Poole workroom, 1944. Tailors at benches, jackets in the lap, a working room rather than a shop floor.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2012-13_in-progress_jacket_and_vest_showing_the_tailoring_process,_by_Kathryn_Sargent_of_Savile_Row.jpg — Kathryn Sargent process jacket. Canvas chest, pad-stitching, and basting left in on a half-finished coat.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bespoke_Tailor_is_taking_measurement.jpg — measuring a client, the reason for the mirror alcove and the tape.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aufnahme_der_pers%C3%B6nlichen_Ma%C3%9Fe_beim_Ma%C3%9Fschneider_Sebastian_Hoofs.jpg — personal measures at a Maßschneider.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:C._Antoniou,_Bespoke_Tailor,_248_Gray%27s_Inn_Road,_WC1_-_geograph.org.uk_-_1229296.jpg — a small London tailor's street front. Scale of one workroom, not a department.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1959_suit,_Kilgour,_French_and_Stanbury_Ltd._Glen_Uruqhart_check,_basketweave_wool_01.jpg — a finished Savile Row suit, used for the navy figure and the cloth-book palette.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savile_Row — bespoke sequence: measure, cut, baste, forward fitting.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bespoke — individual pattern and cloth choice.

Wikimedia pages carry each file's license. This scene does not copy those photographs.
