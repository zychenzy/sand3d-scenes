# Gaps

What this shop still does not have. Links only; nothing below is stored as a binary in git.

- Mirrors are pale emissive panels. They do not reflect the room. A real mirror material was not available in the toon path used here.
- The cutting cape is a tilted card on the headrest, not cloth.
- Work stools are the cafe bar-stool revolve. They have no distinct front. They sit beside the chairs rather than in a counter row.
- Jars are unlabeled jam jars standing in for pomade. The tool cup is a cafe straw holder.
- The flowerpot reads as a grey cylinder from the high camera. Foliage does not carry at that distance. One pot is by the entrance pole, one by the wash wall.
- The book stand is a library lectern used as a magazine rack. It is a bit tall for a waiting table.
- No barber figure, no customer, no hot-towel stack with a real cloth sim, no striped cape.
- Philips and Avon appear only as source titles. The meshes are sand3d rewrites and carry no logos.
- A Dora-branded shampoo (`objaverse-758c2ce72fd44626bacdaf82d05a6233`, https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse) was skipped for character IP.
- The catalog display bookcase and wall shelf were tried and dropped. The bookcase read as a blank block. Retail shelves are procedural instead.
- This Three.js build has `BasicShadowMap`, `PCFShadowMap`, `PCFSoftShadowMap`, and `VSMShadowMap`. It does not define `PCFHardShadowMap`, so hard shadows use basic shadows.
