# North Pit — brief

Team training room, not an arcade and not a cowork office. Daylight comes from the open cutaway (+X, +Z). Ceiling slots are dim warm practicals and do not cast; the hard shadow is the daylight key.

- Five matching desks (TOP, JG, MID, ADC, SUP). Each curved monitor faces the sitter. Chair local +Z is the seat front; `rotationY` near π turns that front toward the screen.
- Shared floor raceway and a spine in each desk gap. Towers sit under the right side of the desk.
- Replay wall on −Z. Draft board to its right. Coach desk and chair on the right, same facing, set back so the coach looks over the row at the replay.
- Acoustic panels and a wood-slat diffuser on the back and left walls. Headset rack and hydration nook on the left. Analyst bench in the forecourt, seat front toward the wall.
- Circulation is the aisle in front of the chairs (clear zone `aisle`) and the walk past the coach (`coach_walk`). The slots between desks are service gaps, not paths.

See `layout.json` for metres, cameras, and the asset paths.
