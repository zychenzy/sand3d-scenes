# Calligraphy classroom

A working practice room, not a generic classroom and not a Western painting studio.

## Room

12.2 m wide × 9.6 m deep × 3.36 m high. Back wall and left wall are closed. The front and the right stay open so the camera can stand outside the shell.

- Left wall: two lattice windows, a sill, bamboo outside the glass, a side cabinet, and a bench in the window aisle.
- Back wall: wainscot, a cinnabar name board, a large teaching sheet (stroke-order marks), a 山 scroll and a 水 scroll, and a row of pinned studies.
- Platform: low wood dais. Demonstration table and the teacher’s chair sit on it. The teacher faces the room.
- Floor: planks, a dark border, and an indigo runner with cinnabar edges down the center aisle.
- Ceiling: beams and five paper lanterns. The banker’s lamp on the demonstration table is a practical.

## Writing floor

Six tables, 3.15 m × 0.70 m × 0.75 m. The depth is short on purpose so a seated writer can reach the far edge. Three seats per table, about a metre apart, all on the room side of the table, facing −Z.

Each station has a felt mat. Most have paper, an inkstone, a water dish, and a brush (lying, or standing in a pot). Variants differ: a large sheet, a partial 永, a finished sheet with a seal, a gridded sheet, 山, 水, and one cleared mat. One chair is pulled back 14 cm. One chair is yawed about 4 degrees and still faces the wall.

The dining-chair seat top is 0.4165 of the grounded mesh. At 1.05 m overall the seat is 0.437 m, 0.31 m under the writing top.

## Service side

Two bookcases against the back-right wall, openings toward the room, filled with slipcases, a paper roll, and flat stacks. A wash stand with a celadon basin, a chest, a drying line of six sheets in front of an ink-landscape screen.

## What is not in this room

No monitors, no chalkboard, no school desks, no easels, no people.
