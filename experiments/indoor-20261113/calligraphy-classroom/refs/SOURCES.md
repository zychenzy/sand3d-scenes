# Sources

Links only. No reference photos, candidate dumps, or Hugging Face bulk downloads are stored in git.

## Room type

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_calligraphy — the four treasures (brush, ink, paper, inkstone) and the practice of copying a model.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Treasures_of_the_Study — brush, ink stick, paper, inkstone, plus the water dropper, paperweight, and seal.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_script — the 永 character as the usual stroke-order model (永字八法).
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inkstone — the stone and the well.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Calligraphy_classrooms — public photos of shared writing tables, felt, and work hung to dry. Not copied into the tree.
