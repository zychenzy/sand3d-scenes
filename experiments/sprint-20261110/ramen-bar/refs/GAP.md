# Gaps

Closed in this scene with procedural meshes, because the catalog has no ramen bowl, chochin, noren, or wood counter:

- Hinoki counter, steel range, hood, duct, ladles, sink.
- Stock pots with animated steam, four varied ramen bowls, tea cups, chopsticks.
- Back prep, bowl stacks, soy bottles, left fridge, right dish cabinet.
- Noren, sign, red and paper lanterns, lattice window, menu board, scroll, genkan, floor runner.

Still approximate:

- Stools are the catalog metal pedestals. They have no back and no forward axis; the guest side of the counter is what makes them face the pots. A wood ramen stool is not in the bake set.
- The thermos cap is modeled beside the bottle in the source program, so the flask reads as a bottle plus a small cap, not a sealed carafe.
- Jam jars stand in for chili oil. Canisters stand in for sesame. Neither is a labeled condiment bottle.
- Pendant shades are the cafe lamp, hung with an extra cord up to the beam. Paper lanterns are procedural.
- Menu and noren text use the system CJK font when the browser has one. The red circle, price column, and color chips still read if the glyphs do not.
- Contact shadows are decals. The hard shadow is one directional light. Point lights at the lanterns and hood are local and can still flatten a toon band if pushed brighter.
- No people, no animated cook, no real fluid simmer.
