# Quill & Spine — scene spec

Independent bookshop, 10.8 × 8.2 × 3.58 m, metres, cutaway (back wall −Z, left wall −X).

## Zones

- **Window reading.** Divided shop window on the back wall, wood apron, two upholstered benches facing the room, a reading table, two dining chairs whose fronts face the table and the glass (−Z), banker lamps, an open book.
- **Display island (focal).** Butcher-block table on a rug, face-out and stacked books, a floor lectern facing the entrance (+Z), pendant above.
- **Checkout.** Paneled counter facing the entrance, cash register and POS toward the customer, bookseller's chair on the shop side facing the counter (+Z).
- **Stacks.** Poetry bay (back left) with a low face-out ledge, fiction run on the left wall, new-arrivals bay (back right) with a stepped angled shelf, and a double-sided gondola in the left aisle.

## Orientation

Bookcase openings are the model's local −Z. `rotationY = π` opens them into the room; left-wall cases use `−π/2` so they open toward +X.

Dining chairs and the cash register face local +Z. Window chairs and the register use that front toward the table, the glass, or the customer.

## Light

Warm key from the entrance side (hard shadow), cool window fill, hemisphere, pendant and banker point lights, emissive bulbs kept through the Toon gradient. Contact discs sit under floor furniture. Unlit swaps to basic materials and keeps maps, glass, and lamp glow.
