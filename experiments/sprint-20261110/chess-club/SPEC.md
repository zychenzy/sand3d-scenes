# Chess clubhouse — brief

A cutaway club room in warm oak and plaster, not a second copy of the board-game hall.

Two zones:

1. **Match floor.** Featured table on a green rug with a middlegame (one knight each already traded, the pair sitting beside the board) and a chess clock. A second table, turned ninety degrees, holds a full starting position, its own clock and two chairs. A reading perch and floor lamp sit toward the open side so that foreground is not bare floor.
2. **Analysis and viewing nook.** A timber screen with a cased opening divides the back-left. Inside: three bookcases, a lectern, an analysis board showing the same position as the “game of the day” wall diagram, a banker lamp, and two chairs side by side facing that table. One chair sits in the doorway and another just outside, both turned toward the featured match.

Also in the room: wainscot panels, chair rail, picture rail, cornice, ceiling beams, a panelled timber screen, two windows with curtains, a sideboard of trophies, sconces, a club sign, a wall clock, and two printed demonstration boards (the match and the game of the day).

Room: 9.6 × 7.6 × 3.28 m. Units metres. Back wall −Z, left wall −X, cutaway +X and +Z.

Chairs use the dining-chair GLB. Rotation 0 faces +Z. Every placed chair stores a `lookAt` and is yawed so its forward axis points at that target.
