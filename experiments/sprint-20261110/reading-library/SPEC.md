# Reading library — brief

A tall public reading room, richer than the v1 `library/` box of repeated white cases and two identical tables.

Architecture: floor-to-cornice built-in shelving with unequal bays, a ladder and a center niche; three window alcoves of different widths; layered wall (base, wainscot, chair rail, pictures, cornice) and a coffered partial ceiling so the cutaway still reads.

Furniture: communal oak table, square study tables, a carrel study desk, a quiet study desk, one index desk, dining chairs facing those tops, banker lamps on the tops, pendants, a window bench, two loaded book carts, a catalog lectern.

Light: cool window spill and a cool directional from the alcoves; warm point lights at lamps, pendants, and sconces; hard shadows; contact blobs under furniture; emissive lamp shades and bulbs.

Room: 13.2 × 9.2 × 4.45 m. Units metres. Cutaway opens toward +X and +Z.
