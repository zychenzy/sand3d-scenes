# Alpine lodge common room

One indoor common room, not a lobby diagram. The fire is the thing you walk toward. Everything else is a place to sit, read, or drop gear.

Room: 10.6 × 7.8 × 3.52 m. Back wall −Z, left wall −X, cutaway open on +X and +Z. Units metres.

Zones:

- Fireplace inglenook on the back wall, left of centre. Stone breast, hearth, mantel, timber posts.
- Communal trestle table on a bordered rug, with a bench on the near side and dining chairs that face the top.
- Reading nook in the cool window: two wooden benches as a window seat, a sofa turned toward the fire, a lamp, books.
- Boot and gear run along the back wall to the right of the wood store: pegs, coats, bench, boots, skis.

Axes: `dining_chair` looks along local +Z. `sofa_solid` looks along local −Z. `piano_bench` is long on local X.
