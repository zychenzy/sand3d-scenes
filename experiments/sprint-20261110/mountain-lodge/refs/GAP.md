# GAP — mountain lodge

The Commons links in `SOURCES.md` show carved stone, ironwork, and real textiles. This room is a toon cutaway, so several of those stay simplified.

Closed in this scene:

- Fireplace, hearth, mantel, inglenook posts, and a wood store are built as stone courses and timber, not a single box.
- Communal table is a trestle with an uneven mix of bench and chairs. Chairs use the dining-chair GLB and face the top (local +Z).
- Reading nook uses the piano-bench GLB as a window seat, the sofa turned toward the fire (local −Z), the green bankers lamp, and a book wall.
- Boot wall has pegs, coats, a timber bench, boots, skis, and the wall-shelf GLB.
- Rugs are layered: dark underlay, field, border, a second rug overlapping, and a sheepskin.
- Cool window light (key + a blue point at the glass) against the warm fire point and spot. Bulbs and flames are emissive so they stay bright in toon. Contact discs sit under the seats.

Still open:

- Stone is stacked boxes, not a carved Timberline breast. The arch is a flat lintel. Medium.
- Rug pattern is flat color bands, not a woven textile. Low.
- The reading lamp is the green library bankers lamp. It reads as a lamp, not a lodge lantern. Low.
- Pendant GLBs are the cafe copper shade. Cords are extended with a thin cylinder so they reach the beams. Low.
- Coats, boots, and skis are block silhouettes. They read at room scale and fall apart up close. Medium.
- Dining chairs are a generic oak chair, not a lodge stick-back. Low.
- One hard shadow map from the cool key, plus blob AO. The fire spot does not cast, so flame-side contact is from the fill and the discs. Medium.
- Cutaway has no front or right wall. The gear zone is on the back wall so it stays in frame. By design.
