# Quiet shared study loft

A cutaway of a shared academic loft. Units are metres. The open sides are +X and +Z.

## Two zones

- **Hall** (double height). One communal oak table on an indigo rug, six chairs pulled up to the long sides, three banker lamps, two pendants. A pair of bookcases stands as a browse pier on the right, with a lectern in front of them, clear of the table aisle.
- **Loft** (walk surface at 2.66). Three carrels along the back wall, deliberately not identical: overhead shelves, a side bookcase, and a lower shelf under a taller window. Chairs face the back wall (toward the desk). A 1.02 m walkway runs behind the chairs to a 1.05 m guard.

The undercroft below the loft is a stack aisle: built-in oak shelves on either side of a window seat. It is circulation, not a second copy of the carrels.

## Stair

15 risers of 177 mm, 14 goings of 270 mm (2R+G ≈ 625 mm). Clear width 1.00 m between stringers. It climbs toward the back wall and the last riser is the loft edge, so the flight is entirely in the tall volume (soffit headroom 2.40 m, not over the stairs). Handrail 0.90 m. Guard baluster clear gap 92 mm. The aisle from the stair's open side to the table is 1.66 m. The approach in front of the first riser is 1.42 m.

Posts under the front edge: one at the stair newel, then two more so the slab is not floating. Joists show in the soffit. A tall window on the left wall is in front of the stair, so the handrail stays on solid wall.

## Facing

Dining-chair yaw 0 faces +Z. Table chairs on the north side use yaw 0; south side and all carrel chairs use yaw π so they face the table or the desk.

## Light

Hard key from the open corner, cool fill from the left window, a warm point on each banker lamp and pendant. Lamp bulbs (`light_tube`, pendant `bulb`) stay unlit-white so they read as emitters in toon mode. Contact discs sit under the table, chairs, posts, and pier.
