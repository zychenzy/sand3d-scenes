# GAP — study loft

Closed in this scene:

- Two walking levels, with the loft slab carried on posts and joists rather than a floating plane.
- Stair rise, going, width, handrail, and guard height are explicit and checked in `tests/circulation.test.mjs`.
- Built-in carrel desks and undercroft shelves. The HF display case is only the browse pier (two bays), so the back wall is not a repeat of the library scene.
- Task lamps per carrel plus pendants over the communal table. Bulb meshes stay bright.

Still open:

- The housed stair is a solid block with a straight skirt, not a cut stringer with visible triangular treads. The side profile reads, but it is heavier than a framed wood stair.
- HF bookcases are display carcasses. Procedural spines sit on the shelf line; they are not individually modeled volumes inside a real rebate.
- The banker-lamp shade is a closed green shell. The emissive tube is inside it, so the glow is a bright core rather than a pool of light on the page. Point lights do the illumination.
- No people, no exterior beyond the cutaway, and no second stair. The right and front are open on purpose.
- Contact discs are transparent radial patches, not a screen-space AO pass.
