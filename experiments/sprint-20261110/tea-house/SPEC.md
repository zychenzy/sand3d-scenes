# Contemporary tea house

One room, two jobs: guests sit down to taste, and someone prepares tea behind them.

## Zones

- **Tasting.** One long oak table on a low walnut dais. Six matching chairs face the top (two along each long side, one at each end). The host end is the west chair, toward the counter. A cloth runner, iron kettle, clay pot, caddies, and a loose cluster of bowls sit on the top — a flight, not a place-setting grid.
- **Preparation.** The cafe counter mesh is scaled shallow (about 0.82 m deep) and retinted to a stone top over a dark base. Shelves, jars, a thermos, and a paper andon sit on and above it. One stool faces the counter. Millwork continues along the left wall toward a backless waiting bench.

## Architecture

Back wall is plaster with a real window opening (sill, head, jambs, glass). A paper-and-slat screen stands in front of the glass; a second, tighter lattice stands further into the room. The right side is a short garden walk: angled freestanding screens, pots, a stone basin, and a window bench. The left wall has a shallow niche, a tea list, and a cabinet run. The ceiling is a partial soffit with beams over the table and the counter. Pendants and paper lanterns are emissive and have point lights.

The front and right of the shell stay open so the cutaway reads.

## Palette

Oak floor, walnut dais, plaster walls, indigo cloth, celadon and clay glazes, warm lamp light. Hard four-step toon, hard shadows, contact discs under floor furniture.
