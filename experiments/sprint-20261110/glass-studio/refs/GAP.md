# Gaps

The catalog has no glory hole, lehr, blowpipe, marver block, cullet, or art-glass vessel. Those are procedural meshes in `glass.js`.

- Finished “clear” glass is opaque smoke grey. Real transparency would sort badly under the toon path, so every vessel is solid colour with a dark mouth and a light lip.
- The gallery shelf, lehr, grinder, and blowpipe rack are procedural. The catalog has no lehr, glory hole, or art-glass mesh.
- `museum_bench` is a flat bench, not a purpose-built gaffer’s chair. Rails and the loaded pipe are added in front of it. The marver stool is a procedural seat, not a catalog stool.
- Jam jars stand in for a few purchased colour pots. The rest of the frit is procedural.
- There is no gaffer figure. The hot pipe on the rails is the stand-in for work in progress.
- Cullet is low-poly icosahedra, heaped above the bin rims so the colour reads. It is not scanned glass.
