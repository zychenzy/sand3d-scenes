# Gaps

What the photos have that this room only approximates.

- The booth GLB is a complete facing pair with a yellow laminate top. Back-to-back means two of those units with their outer bench backs almost touching. There is no separate high-back bench asset, so the joint is the models' own backs rather than a built banquette spine.
- Counter stools are the cafe round pedestal stool. They face the counter, but they are not chrome fountain stools with a ring foot. The foot rail is procedural, on the counter.
- The cash register is the ornate cafe register, older than a 1950s diner register.
- Coffee service is two thermos bottles, not a dedicated urn. Condiments and the pie case are procedural cylinders.
- Neon is a canvas tube drawing on an emissive toon material. There is no bloom and the intensity stays low, so it reads as a sign rather than a night exterior.
- Menu and "today" boards use the system sans (Inter / Noto). They are painted planes, not modeled letterforms.
- No exterior chrome diner skin. This is the interior cutaway only. The left wall has a window and a door; the front is open for the camera.
- No freestanding partitions. The pass is a shelf and heat lamps over the counter.
