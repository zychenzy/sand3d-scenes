# Neighborhood diner — spatial spec

Units are metres. The room is 12.8 wide (X), 9.8 deep (Z), 3.16 high. The +Z side is the open cutaway. The camera looks in from that opening.

## Zones

1. **Booth bank** (left). Four diner-booth models in two rows. Each model is already a facing pair (bench, laminate table, bench). Within a row the two models are turned ±90° so their outer backs meet with about a 2 cm gap. That is the back-to-back joint. A window runs along the left wall beside the bank.
2. **Tables** (center aisle). Two long dining tables, scaled to about 2.08 × 1.20, each with six wooden chairs. Chairs on the long sides and both ends face the tabletop. The cross aisle between the tables is about a metre.
3. **Counter** (right wall). A tiled counter with a laminate top, chrome nose, and foot rail. Five round stools sit on the dining side and face the counter. Behind the top is a galley: heat-lamp shelf (the pass), ticket slips, plates, coffee urns, a pie case, and a cash register at the entry end. The menu board is on the right wall above the pass. None of this is a freestanding screen.

## Circulation

- Window gap on the left of the booths, about 0.8 m.
- Aisle between the booth bank and the west chairs, about 0.9 m.
- Aisle between the east chairs and the stools, about 0.9 m.
- Entry at the front-left: door, mat, coat hooks, a small "today" board, and one plant. The front of the room stays open for the camera.

## Materials and light

Checker floor (ivory / charcoal, 0.5 m), subway wainscot, cherry rail, chrome window bars. Toon materials keep the 4-step gradient. Tile and menu canvases supply the surface pattern. Contact discs sit under the furniture. The key light enters through the open front; it is not placed outside a solid wall. Pendant bulbs, the neon wordmark, the heat lamps, and the counter tube are emissive. Neon intensity stays under 0.55 and there is no bloom pass.

## Facing

Dining-chair forward is local +Z. Stools are round; their +Z is aimed at the counter (+X). Booth models have no single front; the back-to-back test uses the outer bench ends.
