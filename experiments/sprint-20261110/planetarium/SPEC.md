# Star Chamber — brief

Intimate indoor planetarium, shown as a cutaway (back wall and left wall only).

Two zones:

1. **Dome chamber.** Partial hemisphere, open toward the foyer and the cut side. Horseshoe of one seat type (old theatre chair) on the floor and on a low outer step. Seats face a brass star projector at the centre. Entry aisle from the foyer, a ring path between the rows, and a radial gap in the outer row.
2. **Foyer.** Stone floor, lower soffit, arch at the threshold. Saturn and Jupiter on plinths, a tabletop orrery on a vitrine, a filled bookcase, lectern, bench, banker’s lamp, and pendant lamps.

Room: 11.0 × 12.4 × 5.5 m. Dome radius 4.45 m, centre (−0.6, −1.45).

Palette: navy bowl, brass ribs and arch, warm plaster, stone foyer, burgundy seats from the chair asset.
