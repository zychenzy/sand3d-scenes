# Small blackbox theatre

One room, two working zones.

**Stage.** Raised plank deck, stage-right wing with steps down to the house. A plaster interior set (door ajar, window practical, table, two chairs facing that table, rug, lamp) sits inside black masking. Wine traveler and teaser are half open on the opening; black legs mask the wings. An overhead grid (FOH, two electrics, house pipe) carries PAR and ellipsoidal fixtures with amber, rose, steel-blue, and green gels.

**House.** Three ranked rows, four seats stage-left of the aisle and three stage-right, on two risers. Theatre chairs face the stage. Outer seats toe in toward the stage focus. Aisle runner and a rear rail with a gap.

**Prop bay.** Upstage left, beside the deck: half-wall and soffit, prep desk with lamp, monitor, and a chair facing the desk, wall shelf, prompt stand, costume rack, crates, cable coil.

Room 10.2 × 10.8 × 4.65 m. Cutaway keeps the back and left walls. No second seat type in the house.
