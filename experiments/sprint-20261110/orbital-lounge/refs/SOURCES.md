# SOURCES — orbital lounge

Links only. Reference photos are not stored in git.

- [ISS-42 interior view from the Cupola](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ISS-42_Interior_view_from_the_International_Space_Station%27s_Cupola_module.jpg) — NASA, public domain. Used for the port surround, the limb as the thing you look at, and restrained interior lighting rather than a glass wall.
- [Unity module interior, stowage and food canisters](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Unity_module_interior.jpg) — NASA, public domain. Used for locker-bay storage and a small galley against a technical wall.
- [Category: Interior of Unity (ISS module)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Interior_of_Unity_(ISS_module)) — crew meals at a shared table inside a node, which is the mess zone.

The scene is not a replica of either module. The Cupola is the reason the window is one framed port. Unity is the reason the room is a communal mess with storage, not a cockpit.
