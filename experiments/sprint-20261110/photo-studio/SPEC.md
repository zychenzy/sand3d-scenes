# Photography studio — brief

A working stills bay, not an empty white room with a table in the middle.

- Seamless corner cyclorama (back wall, left wall, quarter-round cove, flat sweep).
- Backdrop rail with four paper rolls over the cove; extra rolls stored on a floor rack.
- Goalpost truss framing the sweep, with an overhead octa and two PAR cans.
- Key C-stand with a square softbox, fill stand, and a strip as a hair light.
- Camera dolly on rails pointed at a dress form on a tape mark.
- Editing desk on the right: chair faces the monitor, banker's lamp and mug on the top.
- Changing corner at the front-left: parted curtain, bench, mirror, garment rack.

Measurable poses, cameras, and room size are in `layout.json`.
