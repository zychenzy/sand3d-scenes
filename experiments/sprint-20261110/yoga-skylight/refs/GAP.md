# Gaps

The catalog has no yoga mat, bolster, block, curtain, or glazed-roof asset. Those are procedural meshes in `yoga.js`: thickened mats with a head band, cylinders for rolls and the bolster, folded boxes for blankets, and a timber lantern with flat glass panes.

Other limits:

- The bench is a museum bench (oak seat, crossed steel legs), tinted warmer. It is backless, so either side is a seat. It is not a locker-room bench.
- Pots are the cafe flowerpot. Leaves are a few boxes and cylinders, not a scanned plant.
- Pendant emissive bulbs are extra spheres. The GLB shade itself is not an emitter.
- Contact grounding is a radial decal plus a hard shadow map, not screen-space AO.
- The toon gradient's darkest stop is lifted (about 0.34) so shadowed timber does not crush to black.
- Glass is a transparent toon pane. It does not refract.
- No people, and no cloth simulation. The curtain is overlapping slabs gathered to one jamb.
