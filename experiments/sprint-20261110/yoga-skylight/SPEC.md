# Skylight yoga studio

A 9.2 × 8.0 × 3.4 m cutaway. Front (+Z) and right (+X) are open. Back and left are plaster with a timber rail, base, and vertical slats.

## Zones

**Practice floor.** A dark timber border sets the mat area. The instructor mat sits on a 100 mm platform against the back, head band toward the class. Three student mats sit with at least 0.6 m of clear edge gap: two under the lantern, one in the foreground cut. Blocks, a folded blanket, and one bolster are the only mat props.

**Changing and storage.** A shallow timber niche in the left wall holds a bench, round mirror, hanging robe, and a linen curtain gathered to the south jamb. Open cubbies on the back wall hold rolled mats, folded textiles, blocks, and a bolster. A thermos sits on the cubby top.

## Skylight

The focal structure is a pitched lantern, ridge parallel to X, cut on the near edge so the slope, rafters, cheeks, and baffle read in section. Glass does not cast shadows. A few baffle beams do. Flat ceiling remains over the aisle and the altar.

## Shading

Local `scene_lib.js` keeps the four-step toon gradient, with the shadow stop lifted so timber color stays visible. Hard shadow map, warm skylight key, two pendant bulbs and the altar candle are emissive. Mats, the bench, cubbies, and pots get contact pads.

## Palette

Plaster, three oak slat tones, clay, sage, sand, mauve, linen, bronze. No extra accent colors.
