# Aquarium visitor hall

A small public aquarium gallery, not a domestic room. One large planted tank is the reason for the visit. A second zone is a niche walk of three smaller live exhibits. Circulation enters through the open cutaway, passes a bench that faces the glass, and can peel left along the niches.

## Room

12.0 × 8.4 × 4.0 m. Back wall and left wall only (cutaway). Units metres.

## Zones

1. **Kelp forest tank** — stone plinth, mullioned glass, sand, stacked rocks, ribbon kelp, broad leaves, driftwood, and several fish silhouettes (angelfish, garibaldi, discus, rockfish, wrasse, needlefish, ray, a loose school of tetras). Cool point lights sit inside the volume. A rail keeps visitors off the glass. The plinth carries the tank title.
2. **Niche walk** — three different openings on the left wall (garibaldi, señorita, rockfish), each with its own rock or kelp, one fish, glass, and a plaque. A habitat diagram and a staff door occupy the back wall beside the main tank.

## Furniture

- One shared museum bench, `rotationY = π/2`, long axis parallel to the glass so a sitter faces the tank. Seat height 0.46 m.
- Two shared flowerpots with procedural foliage flank the plinth.
- Lectern, stanchion rail, and wayfinding pylon are built for this hall.

## Light

Dim slate room, hard key from the open side, tank and niches carrying a restrained cyan glow. No bloom, no outline. Emissive caps on the soffit spots. Unlit mode keeps the same colors, with glow mixed into the flat color so the tank still reads.

## Palette

Slate walls and floor, sand and brass as the warm notes, teal water, one saturated orange fish as the accent.
