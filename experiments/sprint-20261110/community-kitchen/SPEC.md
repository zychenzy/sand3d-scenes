# Community kitchen classroom

Cutaway teaching kitchen, metres, Toon-hard (4-step gradient, hard shadows) with an unlit flat pass.

## Room

10.6 wide × 7.8 deep × 3.12 high. Closed back wall and left wall. A return wall on the right closes only the cook line (`z` to -1.15). Front and classroom-right stay open.

Floor changes at the cook line: tile in the working zone, oak planks in the classroom, with a wood threshold. A rug marks the entry path and stops before the stools.

## Zones

**Island (focal).** Center `(0, -1.38)`, 2.92 × 1.12, top at 1.04. Oak carcass, butcher top, 0.16 overhang. Stools on the south (class) side and both ends, facing the block. North side is open for the teacher. Three pendants, warm point lights, emissive bulbs.

**Cook line.** Counter from x -3.55 to 3.25, front at z -3.12, height 0.92. Left to right: pantry bookcase, prep with open shelves, four-burner range and hood (one burner lit), sink under a window, espresso coffee niche. Fridge in the right return corner. Aisle between island north edge and counter front is about 1.18 m.

**Tasting.** Square table at `(-3.7, 1.9)`, height 0.75. Two dining chairs face the tabletop. Lesson board on the left wall, writing surface toward the room. Window and sill herbs beside the table.

## Circulation

Entry over the rug, then either side of the island. The teacher aisle is clear at `z ≈ -2.5`. West end stool and tasting table stay about a metre apart. Bins sit in the gap between the counter and the fridge, not in the aisle.

## Shading

MeshToonMaterial + shared 4-step gradient. Emissive bulbs, hood strip, and the lit burner stay bright in unlit mode. Contact discs ground the island, stools, chairs, fridge, and table. No bloom.
