# Passenger ship salon — brief

A complete cutaway lounge on a passenger ship, not a furnished box.

Architecture: timber ribs on a tumblehome profile, crown about 2.74 m, sole at 0. Six brass portholes at 1.34 m (seated/standing eye), diameter 0.40 m. Deckhead planks follow the same curve. One long side is the cutaway.

Zones:

1. Aft chart alcove — focal framed chart, chart chest with books, bookcase, atlas stand, reading chair facing the chart, square communal table (0.75 m) served by a built-in settee.
2. Forward mess — built-in settee and a 1.8 m communal table (0.76 m) with chairs facing the cloth.

Seat height 0.48 m. Settees have a plinth to the sole. A compass inlay occupies the bay between the settees. Practical lights: brass picture light on the chart, caged deckhead lights, hull sconces, two amber pendants, a brass reading lamp on the chart table.

Units: metres. Shading: Toon hard (4-step gradient, hard shadows) and Unlit.
