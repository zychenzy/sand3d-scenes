# GAP — indoor swimming hall

Closed in this scene:

- Recessed basin, freeboard, sloping tiled floor, waterline, and course lines so depth reads through the surface.
- Three lanes: floor Ts, wall targets, and float ropes. Steps at the shallow right lane, ladder at the deep left lane.
- Dado, mullions, gable glass, timber trusses, and a roof that stops so the lounge stays open to the cutaway.
- Changing niche between two truss bays: shelf, bench, towels, mirror, grate.
- Lounge benches and two cafe pendants over the dry deck only. Underwater fixtures are emissive strips plus short point lights.

Still open:

- Water is a translucent toon surface with a low wave, not refraction, caustics, or a simulation.
- No starting blocks and no diving. The hall is a 10 m learner / club basin, not 25 m or 50 m.
- Catalog has no teak deck chair, lifeguard tower, or lane reel. Benches are the oak museum bench; lamps are the cafe pendant.
- The niche is a screened bay, not a locker room: no showers, cubicles, or lockers.
- Glazing does not transmit the shadow map. Cool fill stands in for light through the glass. The hard key comes from the open cutaway.
