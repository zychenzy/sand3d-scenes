# Intimate indoor swimming hall

A cutaway municipal hall, not a stadium. The water is the room.

- Basin about 9.9 m by 5.4 m, three lanes. Floor slopes from 1.9 m at the back (−Z) to 1.2 m at the lounge end (+Z). Water sits 0.1 m below the deck.
- Glazed walls above a tiled dado on the back and left. Timber trusses and a partial roof; the right side and the shallow end are the cutaway.
- Two zones: the lap basin (ropes, ladder, pace clock, depth marks) and the dry lounge (benches, pendants, steps) with a changing niche in the bay between the trusses at z = 0.8 and z = 2.8.
- Catalog benches are oak-and-steel, long axis along model Z. Lounge benches are yawed so that axis runs across the deck. The niche bench stays along Z, against the outer wall.

Measurable poses, cameras, and lights are in `layout.json`.
