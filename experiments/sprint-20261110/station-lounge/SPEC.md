# Northgate station waiting hall

A finished heritage concourse, not an empty shell. About 12.6 × 8.8 m, vault crown near 5.4 m.

## Zones

- **Waiting nave.** An oxblood aisle runner runs from the approach to the departure board. Window benches sit parallel to the arched platform wall (people can face the glass). Four hall benches are turned so their long sides face the board. A lectern, a side table, and an alterations board break the rows up.
- **Booking office.** Back-left arched hatch with a timber counter, brass grille, register, lamp, and one chair in front of the window facing the grille. Warm light stays in this niche. Left-luggage is a smaller arched bay on the back-right, not a third room.

## Focal point

Round clock centered on the aisle, enamel `NORTHGATE` band, and a printed departure sheet on the stone pier between the two arches.

## Light

Cool directional wash through the left arches (a carriage and canopy read outside the glass). Warm point lights at the booking pendant, three hall pendants, and a picture light over the board. Hard shadow map from the open side. Contact discs under benches, columns, and bags.

## Furniture axes

Museum bench local +Z is the long axis. Hall benches use `rotationY = π/2`. The booking chair uses `rotationY = π` so its front faces −Z, toward the ticket window.
