# Meeple & Bean

A social boardgame cafe, not a second copy of `boardgame/` (three empty square tables) or `cafe/` (round coffee tables).

Zones:

- **Play floor.** One 1.88 × 0.98 m trestle table (hex map, six chairs), one 1.28 m square (track game, four chairs), one 1.22 m round card table (four chairs), one 0.98 m go board (two chairs). Rugs mark each group. Chairs use the dining-chair GLB, whose backrest is local −Z, and face the tabletop.
- **Library wall.** Display-case GLBs along the back and left walls, filled with face-out game boxes on the measured shelf heights. A low feature table and a front crate hold more boxes.
- **Service bar.** Millwork counter, five stools facing the counter, espresso machine, register, pastry case, backbar shelves with bottles.
- **Teaching nook.** Chalkboard and lectern on the left wall, a small table with a lesson layout, banker's lamp, two chairs facing the board and the demo.

Pendants hang over every play table and along the bar. Spot lights make a warm pool on each surface. The key light is kept modest so those pools cross a toon band.
