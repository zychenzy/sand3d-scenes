# Gaps

Catalog has no potter's wheel, kiln, ware rack, or glaze tile. Those are authored in `ceramics.js` as lathed vessels, brick courses, and practical lights. They are toon stand-ins, not scanned equipment.

Reused as symlinks (not duplicated): wedging table, dining chair, bar stool at the glaze bench, library lamp, pendant lamps, museum plinths, wall shelf, jam jars (oxide jars), mugs, flowerpot.

Throwing stools are procedural. The catalog stool is bar height and is used only at the glaze bench.

Plinths are the museum column bake, scaled per piece. Pots sit on the top face.

No measured plan of a specific studio. Circulation and heights are workshop-typical (wheel head near 0.52 m, wedging top 0.76 m, glaze bench 1.02 m, bar stool 0.78 m) rather than surveyed.

Window is a glazed panel on the back wall, not a cut opening. Brick courses are instanced skins on a solid mass.
