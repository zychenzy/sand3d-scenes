# Afterglow Records — spec

Cutaway shop, 11.2 × 8.4 × 3.2 m. Origin at floor centre. Back wall −Z, left wall −X, open on +X and +Z. Units are metres.

## Zones

**Listening (left).** Oxblood rug. Sofa against the left wall, rotation −π/2, faces +X onto a coffee table with a turntable, headphones and a banker’s lamp. Two monitor speakers flank the table and face the sofa. Forward of that, a square table audition desk; the dining chair sits on the +X side, rotation −π/2, facing the desk, lamp, turntable and a combo amp. Foam panels on the wall behind the sofa, wood-slat diffuser on the back-left, bass trap in the corner, a hung cloud overhead.

**Retail floor.** Staggered flip bins (Jazz, Soul, Rock) with a lower Club crate and a face-out “New Pressings” rack — not a grid. Two walnut bays on the back-right hold packed spines. A staff-pick plinth near the entrance shows the same sleeve as the wall feature. Delivery crate and a piano bench by the window.

**Cashier (right).** Millwork counter, customer side −X. Register and POS on the top. Guest stool on the shop side facing the counter; staff stool behind it facing back. Pendant overhead.

**Focal wall.** Irregular salon hang of original sleeve graphics, feature frame for “Night Bus,” picture light and the AFTERGLOW sign. Playback speakers sit low in front of the wall.

## Light

Dusk void (`#2a211e`). Warm key from the open front-right, cool fill from the window, point pools at four pendants, both desk lamps, the album wall and the window. Pendant shades and the sign are emissive. Contact discs ground floor objects. Hard shadow map, four-step toon ramp.
