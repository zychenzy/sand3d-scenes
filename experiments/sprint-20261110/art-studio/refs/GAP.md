# Gaps

Catalog meshes used as-is, by relative path. Nothing in this folder is a new GLB.

Built here because the catalog has no sink, pinboard, pigment cabinet, or stretched painting:

- North window wall, sill, radiators, beams
- Wash alcove (basin, faucet, tile splash, towel, bucket)
- Drawer cabinet and the paper-roll bin
- Pinboard studies and the three easel paintings
- Still life on the plinth, palette, drop cloth, floorboards

Compromises:

- Pigment jars are the cafe jam-jar mesh with the glass retinted. Lids stay the original yellow.
- The supply case is the display bookcase. It reads as open shelving, not a flat file.
- The easel GLB’s panel is a blank board. Paintings are thin boxes parented to the front (local +Z), leaned with the panel.
- Wall-shelf end panels in the bake are skewed; they are removed and replaced with side cheeks.
- The banker’s lamp is wide for its height. It sits on the back-right of the table so the palette stays clear.
- Window glass is a faint unlit pane. The bright exterior is a flat sky card outside the wall, so the openings read as daylight without a real exterior.
- Shadow filtering is PCF soft. The toon ramp is still four hard steps. Hard shadows turned the mullions into black stripes across the easels.
- No figure mannequin and no running water. The wash is a station, not a simulation.
