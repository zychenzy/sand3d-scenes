# North-light artist studio

A working daylight studio, not a furniture grid. Two zones share one aisle.

## Painting bay

The left wall is the north window: four sash bays, a deep sill, and radiators under the middle pair. The key light sits outside that wall, low enough that the mullions stripe the floor and the easels. Three easels stand in a staggered cluster on a drop cloth. Their canvases face local +Z (into the room), with a small yaw so the hero turns slightly toward the window.

The hero canvas is the bottle, cloth, and fruit that sit on the plaster stand beside it. A stool is on the canvas side of that easel. The back easel holds a figure block-in; the front easel is a study of the window itself. Notes on the pinboard say the same thing: north light, bottle left, shadow to the right.

## Work, store, wash

The work table is the second station, under a pendant and a lit banker’s lamp. Pigment jars, a palette, and a mug of brushes stay on the top. One chair is tucked on the north side; one is pulled out on the east. Both face the tabletop (dining-chair forward is +Z at rotation 0).

Along the back wall, from the window corner: pinned studies, a drawer cabinet (one drawer open), paper rolls, a supply case, then the wash alcove. The wash has a tiled splash, a basin, a faucet aimed into the room, a towel, and a bucket. A layout desk sits in front of that corner with its chair facing the desk. A second shelf over the sink holds the brushes being rinsed.

## Light

Cool window key, warm lamp and pendant, a low fill from the open cutaway so the canvas faces are not black. Contact blobs sit under the floor furniture. The pendant bulb and the banker’s tube are emissive in toon and stay bright in unlit. Shadow map is soft so the mullion stripes do not posterize into solid black bars; the toon gradient itself stays 4 hard steps.

## Scale

Room 9.6 × 7.6 × 3.42 m. Easels 1.65, table top 0.75, chairs 0.86, supply case 2.05. Jars and mugs are recolored catalog meshes, not new sculpts.
