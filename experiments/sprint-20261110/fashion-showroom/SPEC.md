# Fashion design showroom

A small design-studio showroom, not a shop floor packed with racks.

- **Display court** (back): low stone plinth with a muslin dress form and one dressed mannequin, framed backdrop, swatch board and fabric rolls to the left, a clothing rail to the right.
- **Fitting niche** (left): plaster volume open toward the entry, parted curtain, mirror, bench, one garment on a peg.
- **Consultation** (front right): oak table, two chairs that face it, pendant, bolts and a swatch fan.
- **Pause**: darker stone inset in the middle stays empty. A single viewing bench sits on its edge.

Palette is ivory plaster, limestone, brass, ink, wine, rust, sage, and camel. Toon hard, stepped gradient, hard shadows, contact discs under the heavy pieces, emissive bulbs on the track and pendant.

See `layout.json` for measurable poses and cameras, `refs/GAP.md` for stand-ins.
