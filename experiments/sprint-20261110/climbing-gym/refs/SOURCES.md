# SOURCES — climbing gym

No reference photos are stored in git.

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bouldering — boulder problems are short, ropeless, and protected by pads.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing_gym — indoor walls, graded circuits, mats under the problems.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing_hold — holds are fixed to the wall; color marks the route.

Scale used here, as design assumptions rather than a quoted code clause: gym boulder walls often sit around 4–4.5 m, pads around 30–40 cm, with a landing strip on the order of 2–2.5 m beyond the furthest part of the problem. This scene uses 4.55 m, 34/44 cm, and 2.4 m.
