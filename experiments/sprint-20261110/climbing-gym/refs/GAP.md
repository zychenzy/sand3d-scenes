# GAP — climbing gym

Catalog has no boulder wall, holds, crash pads, lockers, or hangboard. Those are authored in `climbing.js` / `geometry.mjs`.

Reused GLBs (symlinked, not copied):

- Museum bench as the viewing seat, with a rail added on the aisle side.
- Cafe counter, stool, chair, pendant, POS terminal, wall shelf, thermos, and flowerpot for reception.

Still approximate:

- Holds are spheres, boxes, and pinches, not scanned polyurethane shapes.
- Pads are seamed slabs, not upholstered bags.
- The counter is a long cafe bar scaled to a 1.05 m standing desk, so the plan is deeper than a typical front desk.
- Pendant bulbs in the GLB are grey plastic. Emissive bulbs and the aisle stand are local meshes.
- No climbers, brushes, or brushes-and-tickets beyond the route board.
- The hangboard pad is only for that warm-up station. It is not part of the boulder fall zone.
