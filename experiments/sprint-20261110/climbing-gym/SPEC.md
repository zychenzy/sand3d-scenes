# The Quarry — brief

Indoor bouldering gym, metres, toon-hard cutaway. Architecture is the subject: a faceted climbable volume, not a box with gear dropped in.

## Zones

1. **Boulder** along the back wall, about 13.6 m long and up to 4.55 m high under a 5.65 m ceiling.
   - Slab leans away from the climber (~17°).
   - Arete spine stands proud of the slab and the face.
   - Cave lip throws about 1.9 m, then a faceted fang volume.
   - Lower steep wall, then a laid-back downclimb with a ladder.
2. **Fall zone.** Pads 34 cm under vertical and slab ground, 44 cm where the wall overhangs. Each pad runs 2.4 m past the furthest hold or the fang. A yellow/black line marks the aisle edge.
3. **Viewing.** Three museum benches, long axis parallel to the wall, steel-and-wood back rail on the aisle side so sitters face the routes. At least 1.2 m between the proudest pad and the bench.
4. **Reception.** Standing desk (catalog counter, 1.05 m), visitor stools on the open side, staff chair behind the desk, POS, bottles, plant. Lockers and a wall shelf for shoes and chalk. Hangboard with its own thin pad, separate from the boulder mats. Route board on the left wall. Soffit at 3.22 m over this bay only.

## Routes

| color | line |
| --- | --- |
| yellow | slab, mostly jugs |
| green | arete |
| blue | vertical face, smaller crimps |
| orange | left side of the cave |
| pink | roof and the fang |
| white | low traverse across the whole wall |

Open tan holds on the steep section are not a graded circuit. Large tan jugs beside the ladder are the downclimb, not the yellow route.
