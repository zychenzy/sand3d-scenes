# Ash Court — singles court

Metres, Y up. The front wall is the more negative Z. `layout.json` holds cameras, lights, and the furniture that must stay outside the playing box.

## Court

WSF singles, club tin 480 mm (not the 430 mm PSA tin). Length 9.75, width 6.40, lines 50 mm. Front wall: tin top 0.48 with a sloping cap, service line at 1.78, out line at 4.57. Back out line at 2.13. Short line’s near edge is 4.26 from the back wall. Half-court line runs from the short line to the back wall only. Service boxes are 1.60 internal squares behind the short line. The door is 0.86 × 2.115 in the middle of the glass, hinged on −X, and stands ajar into the court. Ceiling underside is 5.78, above the 5.64 clear height; the batten lights sit just under that.

Playing surfaces use one oxblood for lines and the tin cap, a darker tin body, one plaster on both side walls, and a warmer board below the front service line. The floor is one maple. Gallery and reception are outside that rule: stone floor, green reception panel, walnut desk.

## Two zones

The right wall is cut at z = 1.05 so the near court is open; the cut face is dark poche. Behind the glass, a low canopy covers the two benches and a higher gallery ceiling covers the entry. The left wall holds a racket cabinet facing into the gallery. The right niche is the desk, chair (facing +Z, toward the desk), lamp, plant, and the ASH COURT sign.

## Light

Hard toon, no bloom, no outline. A high key from the gallery side models the walls. The court floor does not take that shadow, so the maple stays one surface and the three ceiling battens plus the canopy strips read as the practical lights. Contact blobs sit under the benches, desk, chair, and rack.
