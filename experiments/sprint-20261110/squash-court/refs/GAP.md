# GAP — Ash Court

No squash racket or ball in the catalog. The rack, three rackets, and balls are procedural. The flowerpot bake is an empty pot; a small topiary is built on top of it.

The glass is a tinted `MeshBasicMaterial` (opacity 0.28). It is not reflective and it does not distort the court.

The side-wall out line is a 50 mm ribbon offset vertically between the front and back heights. A strictly perpendicular 50 mm band is about 48.5 mm of vertical rise; the drawn ribbon stays inside that tolerance and keeps clean corners.

The door stands slightly ajar into the court rather than closing flush. The tin is a solid cap, not a sounding board. The 430 mm PSA championship tin is not used. There are no players.

The court floor ignores the key-light shadow on purpose. A partial ceiling shadow was reading as a broken board. Contact under the furniture is the blob planes, not a cast shadow on the maple.
