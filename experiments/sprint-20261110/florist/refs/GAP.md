# GAP — Bloom florist

Closed in this scene:

- Glazed shopfront (stall, mullions, door, awning, sign, sidewalk) instead of an open box.
- Bouquet workbench and wrapping counter as two working zones, with the chair and stool aimed at the work surface.
- Tiered display as the focal fixture.
- One botanical builder per silhouette (rose, tulip, lily, palm, sunflower, monstera, and the rest). The single flowerpot GLB is only the monstera's nursery pot.

Still open:

- Flowers are lathed and spherical toon masses, not scanned blossoms.
- The wrap counter is the cafe bar mesh scaled shorter in depth so it can sit against the wall.
- The window pedestal is the museum case mesh; it reads as a plinth-height case, not a florist pedestal designed for this shop.
- No figure, no refrigerated case, no printed wrapping pattern beyond flat paper colors.
