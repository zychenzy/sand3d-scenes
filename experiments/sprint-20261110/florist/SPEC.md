# Bloom florist — brief

A complete flower shop, not a ring of identical pots.

- Glazed shopfront on the street side: stall riser, mullions, glazed door ajar, awning, BLOOM sign, sidewalk.
- Focal tiered wooden display, steps facing the door, each tier a different botanical silhouette.
- Bouquet workbench (scaled dining table) with the chair facing the tabletop, task lamp, jars, shears, and an in-progress calla arrangement.
- Wrapping station on the service counter: paper rolls, tissue, ribbon pegs, two finished bouquets, register at the door end. The stool faces the counter.

Room is 9.0 × 7.4 × 3.2 m. +Z is the street, +X is the cutaway. See `layout.json` for poses, cameras, and acceptance checks.
