# Architecture studio — brief

One cutaway room, 11.4 × 8.8 × 3.46 m, for a small practice working on a single scheme called North Courtyard.

Zones:

1. Pin-up wall (back, −Z). Drawings, a crit table, a bench, a lectern, an easel, and a presentation model on a plinth.
2. Drafting bay (left-center). Two sloped boards and one digital desk. Chairs face the boards, which face the pin-up.
3. Model shop (right). Standing bench, massing model, flat file, concrete floor.
4. Material library (left wall). Five unequal bays and a binder case. A side door is the entry.

Skylights are sawtooth monitors: glazed face toward the open side of the cutaway, opaque slope back toward the pin-up. The same courtyard massing appears on the model bench, on the plinth, and in the drawings.

Out of scope: people, a plotter, a second project, and edits outside `experiments/sprint-20261110/architecture-office/`.

See `layout.json` for positions, cameras, and facing checks.
