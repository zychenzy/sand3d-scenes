# Boutique screening room

A private house for about thirteen, not a multiplex auditorium.

Focal point: a framed screen on the −Z wall, brass reveal, tied burgundy drapes, a pelmet, and a picture on the screen (horizon, not a blank panel). Speakers sit on plinths just outside the drapes.

House: front row on a low dais, a middle tier, a rear loge. Side aisles share those levels and have their own stair treads, nosings, and puck lights. The loge is two pairs with a gap, not a fifth clone of the front row.

Second zone: an entry niche cut through the left wall at the rear cross-aisle. Lower ceiling, ajar door, coat rail, one-sheet, sconce, rope at the threshold. You enter at loge level and step down toward the screen.

Acoustic panels are irregular fabric blocks on the long wall and beside the proscenium, with a few ceiling baffles. Palette is plum, walnut, and antique brass.

Excluded: dining furniture, a second seat model, a uniform 5×3 grid, a bare box with flat curtains.

Room envelope about 11.2 × 9.6 × 3.9 m, open on +X past the screen return and on +Z.
