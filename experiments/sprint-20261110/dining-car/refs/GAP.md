# Gaps

The saloon is a restaurant carriage, not a copy of `train/` (that coach is a continuous window band, yellow partitions, and near-square tops).

**Used from existing bakes**

- Old Train Seats for the opposing benches. The plinth is solid; the backrest is on local -Z, so pairs are rotated 0 and π.
- Cafe-kit mug, pendant, flowerpot, jam jar, thermos, and straw-holder canister.

**Procedural on purpose**

- HF dining tables in this repo are four-leg squares or trestles (`square_table`, `long_dining`). Saloon tables are one pedestal spine along the carriage, bracketed to the window wall at each end. The galley counter is wall-mounted.
- No usable luggage GLB (earlier bag hits were the wrong object). Cases and a hat box are built meshes on the racks.
- Galley range, pass, racks, piers, and bogies are built meshes. The pendant bulb is not emissive in the GLB, so a small emissive cylinder sits in the shade.

**Known limits**

- Seat proportions come from the bake: at a 1.0 m back the bench is about 1.36 m wide. The car is 3.44 m inside so an aisle still fits.
- The +X wall is cut away above a skirt and below a fascia.
- Gangway doors are relief panels, not open corridors into a second vehicle.
- Curtains and table settings vary by bay; the window pitch itself is regular, which is the carriage rhythm.
- The letterboard is a canvas reading "DINING"; at saloon distance it reads as a frieze more than as type.
- The flowerpot bake is a pot without foliage, so the centerpiece is a vessel.
