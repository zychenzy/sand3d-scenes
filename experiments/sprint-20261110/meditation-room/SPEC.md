# Meditation room

A cutaway room for sitting still. Two zones share one stone floor and do not crowd each other.

## Zones

**Meditation.** A raised stone platform carries four tatami whose seams T-junction (they do not form one cross). Three cushions sit on the south edge of that field — moss host, indigo left, clay right — facing the tokonoma. Center spacing is about 1.18 m and 1.22 m, so a person can pass between seats. The north part of the platform is empty meadow under the skylight.

**Tea.** On the left wall, a stone niche with a timber panel and a short lattice transom. A low walnut table (shared coffee-table GLB, scaled down) stands against the niche. One sand cushion faces the table. On the table: a tray, two bowls, an iron kettle, a folded cloth, and a slim flask. A paper lantern hangs from a bracket on the niche wall.

## Architecture

- Irregular stone pavers, ashlar dado, timber cap rail, plaster above.
- Four posts and a beam frame the platform. The beam stays outside the light well.
- Back-wall lattice: orthogonal grid with a denser diagonal medallion, sitting above the stone.
- Skylight: a flared timber mouth dropped into the room, a narrower hopper rising through the ceiling, a clerestory of slats, a glowing cap, a kumiko grid, and a hanging ring.
- Tokonoma: stone sill, timber jambs, a paper panel with an open ring (not a copied painting), and one branch arrangement to the side.
- One flowerpot, with a short sprig, sits beside the tea table. That and the tokonoma branch are the only plants.

## Scale

Room 8.2 × 6.8 × 3.48 m. Platform top 0.18 m. Tea table top 0.30 m. Cushions about 0.62 × 0.58 m. Walls are cut away on +X and +Z.

## Shading

Toon hard, four-step gradient, one hard key from the skylight, a cool fill from the open side, a spot pooled on the meadow, and warm point lights at the lantern and in the alcove. Contact discs under seats and furniture. The lantern paper and the skylight cap are emissive so they still read when unlit.
