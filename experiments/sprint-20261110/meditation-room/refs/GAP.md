# Gaps

The shared catalog has no zabuton, zafu, tea bowl, tetsubin, andon, kumiko screen, or stone wall. Those are built in `meditation.js` (lathe, extrude, and timber frames), not as boxes standing in for furniture.

Rejected catalog meshes:

- Cafe cup (`cup_mug__6da6f7f87b8d`) is a lidded takeaway cup with an orange sleeve.
- Pendant lamp is an orange cafe shade.
- Wall shelf bakes flat (about 25 mm on Y), so a height-fit would explode its footprint.
- Museum plinth is two boxes and does not add a stone reading the procedural sill lacks.

`sand3d` is not installed in this environment, so no new GLB was baked.

Still rough:

- Foliage is a few stems and flat leaves.
- The kettle handle and spout are simple sweeps.
- Tatami is a bordered slab, not a woven texture.
- Contact shadows are radial discs plus the hard key shadow, not a baked AO map.
- The flask still reads as a thermos if you get close. It is the catalog's only water vessel.
