# Billiards club — brief

One playing table and a small bar / spectator corner. The table is the focal point. Furniture stays outside a brass rectangle that marks a 1.52 m cue swing around the baked 9-foot table (long axis on Z).

Zones:

- Play: pool table, rug, long green shade, chalk, a rail cue, and one leaning cue whose butt stays inside the brass line.
- Back and left walls: scoreboard, night window, two cue racks, house rules.
- Front-left: tinted bar, three stools, backbar bottles, two cafe pendants.
- Front-right: burgundy sofa facing the table (−X), round table with two chairs facing that table.

See `layout.json` for poses, cameras, and the clearance test.
