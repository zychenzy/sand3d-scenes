# Maison Cacao — chocolatier workshop and shop

One room, two zones. Workshop (`z < 0`, wood floor): marble prep island, tempering kettle, cooling rack, packing table, pantry bookcases behind a wood-and-glass partition. Shop (`z > 0`, stone tile): glass display case, wrap counter, tasting table, hero bonbon on a plinth. A brass strip marks the threshold. The front and most of the right wall are open so the camera can read both zones.

See `layout.json` for poses, cameras, and acceptance checks.

Dining chairs face +Z at `rotationY` 0. The register and POS face +Z and are turned to face the customer aisle.
