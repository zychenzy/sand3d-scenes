# Gaps

- Plinth and vitrine bakes are solid columns, not glass cases. The vitrine reads as a walnut pedestal. A small procedural maquette sits on the cap.
- No framed-painting GLB. Canvases are abstract geometry (bars, one disc) inside built frames, with canvas labels. They are not reproductions of existing artworks.
- Sculptures are procedural: stacked totem, open arc, two-slab figure, ring on a rod. They are not scanned works.
- The display case GLB has no authored wood colour, so the scene tints it walnut and fills the shelves with book slabs. Shelf heights assume target height 1.55 m.
- The flowerpot is an empty vessel; one foliage cluster is added above it. There is only one plant.
- The banker’s lamp is a library fitting used on the info desk. Its pale bulb is forced emissive. The shade stays the baked green.
- Spotlight falloff is quantised by the 4-step toon ramp, so pools read as bands rather than smooth cones.
- East and south sides are cut away. Piers, a return wall, and a threshold beam imply the missing enclosure.
- Contact shadows are radial discs, not a screen-space AO pass.
