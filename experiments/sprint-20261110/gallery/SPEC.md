# Contemporary annex — spatial spec

Units are metres. Origin is the room centre. Back wall is −Z, west wall is −X. The cutaway omits the south wall and most of the east wall so the camera can stand outside.

Room: 14.0 × 9.6, height 4.25.

## Zones

**West alcove** (x ≈ −6.9 to −3.5). A full-height partition with a 1.55 m portal (head 2.55). Large vertical work on the west wall, a smaller work on the partition, a horizontal work on the alcove’s back wall. One plinth (stacked totem) south of the portal. One long bench parallel to the west wall, seat facing that painting.

**Nave** (x ≈ −3.4 to 2.0). Back wall holds a wide banded canvas and a tall green canvas, separated by a pilaster. An open steel arc stands left of centre; a plaster figure stands in the gap between the two canvases. One long bench, rotated so its length runs east–west, faces the back wall. Aisles remain on both ends of the bench (about 1.4 m).

**East court** (x ≈ 2.1 to 6.8). Wider portal (head 2.65). Charcoal wall on the back with one near-white canvas (the focal picture). Bronze ring on the centre plinth, small maquette on the walnut pedestal, diptych on the partition. Lower ceiling soffit. One long bench faces the charcoal wall. Two stone piers and a short return wall hold the open east edge. Floor stone is darker than the nave.

**Entry** (south of the oak threshold at z ≈ 1.78). Oak plank floor. Catalog case, stele, info desk, lamp, and plant sit in one row against a low “EDITIONS” screen, inside a wood floor frame. Aisles pass both ends of the screen.

## Circulation

In from the south, across the band, past the stele. Then: around the alcove pier or through its portal; along either side of the nave bench and between bench and plinths; through the court portal to the ring, with the court bench behind you as you face the wall. Portals are empty of furniture. Benches do not sit in doorways.

## Floors

Alcove is cool grey stone, the nave is pale limestone, the court is darker warm stone. The entry is oak boards. A darker oak band marks the threshold, and each portal has an oak saddle.

## Light

Hemisphere plus a steep key. Walls, soffits, and piers do not cast shadow maps, so plaster stays even. Warm spots (no shadow maps) aim at the works. A skylight well over the nave and picture coves at the charcoal wall and the alcove back add the ceiling rhythm. Track rails carry hangers. The desk lamp bulb and skylight glass stay emissive in unlit mode. Toon ramp stays 4-step hard.

## What is procedural

Walls, portals, piers, soffits, frames, abstract paintings, sculptures, desk, stele, books, and foliage. Furniture meshes are the shared GLBs listed in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
