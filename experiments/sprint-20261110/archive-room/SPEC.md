# Archive research room

A municipal reading room for maps and records. Units are metres. Cutaway walls are the back (−Z) and the left (−X); the front and right stay open.

Room 11.6 × 8.6 × 3.5.

## Zones

- **Records wall.** Four oak display cases along the back, plus one on the left wall by the entrance. Procedural cartons and bound volumes sit in the bays. Call plates read BAY A–E.
- **Map drawers.** Three flat-file cabinets on the left wall, fronts toward the room. The county cabinet has one drawer pulled out with a sheet on it. Index shelves hang above the first two cabinets.
- **Document table.** One long oak table west of the runner, with four chairs (two each long side) facing the top, two banker lamps, an unfolded county sheet, a folder, and a rolled map.
- **Reader carrels.** Two study desks under the county wall map, numbered 12 and 14. Chairs sit on the south side and face the desks and the map.
- **Enquiries.** Glazed oak desk on the east side. Glass screen and brass bars face the aisle, with a pass-through. Staff chair is on the east side of the kneehole, facing the glass. IN/OUT trays, blotter, bell, card drawers.
- **Movement.** A green runner is the main aisle from the open front toward the stacks. A standing aisle stays clear in front of the drawers. A cross aisle runs behind the carrel chairs. The trolley is parked in the south-west foreground, off both aisles. A waiting bench sits south of the desk. An atlas lectern stands between the drawer aisle and the table.

## Light

Hard key from the open south-east, cool fill from the left, a picture light on the county sheet, and a short point light at each task lamp and pendant. Lamp bulbs and green shades are emissive. Contact ellipses sit under floor furniture. No bloom.

## Chair axis

Rotation 0 faces +Z. South-side chairs and carrel chairs use π. The staff chair uses −π/2 and faces the desk.
