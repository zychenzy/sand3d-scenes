# Gaps

No flat-file cabinet, book trolley, or glazed service desk exists in the shared GLB set. Those three are built as procedural furniture (drawers, pulls, casters, brass glazing bars), not as stand-ins for models we already have. Desks, chairs, banker lamps, pendants, bookcases, the atlas stand, wall shelves, the bench, and the charter plinth are the shared bakes.

The bookcase GLB’s back panel faces the room (the open side points at the wall). Record boxes and volumes are placed proud of that panel so the bays read as filled; they are not true inset meshes. The case does not cast a shadow, so the spines stay out of a solid black band.

The flowerpot bake is an empty vessel. The plant above it is procedural.

The museum “vitrine” bake is a solid walnut pedestal. The glass hood and the sheet on it are procedural.

Glass is an opacity toon material, not a refractive pane. Contact marks are floor ellipses, not a screen-space AO pass.

Chair seats from the dining-chair bake sit a little low relative to a 760 mm table, consistent with the other rooms that use the same model.
