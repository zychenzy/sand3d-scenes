# GAP — podcast lounge

No broadcast microphone, boom arm, pop filter, mixing console, or desk-height podcast table in the baked GLB set. Those are procedural meshes in `podcast.js` (desk clamp, boom, solid capsule, mesh head, pop-filter disc, fader bank, meter bridge, oval table, brass lamps).

Reused as-is, referenced by relative path (not copied):

- Dining chair — lounge bake, used for every seat
- Monitor — cowork bake, two of them on the mixing desk
- Speaker cabinet — music bake, used as the two control-room monitors
- Wall shelf, pendant, mug, flowerpot — cafe bakes
- Display bookcase — library bake

Known stand-ins:

- No people, so "facing the speaker" is the seat's forward axis, not a face mesh.
- Dining chairs are ladder-backs. The catalog has no lounge armchair.
- The speaker cabinet is a small music-room bake. Each one sits on a wood stand beside the console, with a procedural woofer so the front reads, toed toward the engineer.
- Acoustic foam, slat diffuser, ceiling clouds, credenza, oval table, floor lamp, table lamp, and records are block geometry, not scanned pieces.
- Book rows are colored slabs placed on the bookcase and wall shelves.
