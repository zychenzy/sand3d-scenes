# Podcast lounge — brief

A designed recording lounge, not an empty box and not a dining row.

- Conversation zone: four dining chairs pulled up to a 75 cm oval table. Each seat has headphones on the table and a desk-clamped boom (solid capsule, mesh head, pop filter) aimed at mouth height.
- Control zone: mixing desk with faders, meters, a waveform screen, and two monitors sitting fully on the desk, facing the engineer. The engineer faces the talent and has a clamped mic plus headphones.
- Back wall: bookcase plus wall shelves over a record credenza, with a warm table lamp on the credenza and a brass floor lamp by the set.
- Both solid walls carry foam panels. A slat diffuser and corner trap sit behind the talent. Ceiling clouds hang over the table and the console. A rug sits under the table.

Seat yaw comes from `lookAt` in `layout.json` (`orient.js`). The bookcase treats local −Z as forward; chairs, monitors, and speakers treat local +Z as forward.

See `refs/GAP.md` for what is procedural.
