# Podcast lounge — brief

A designed recording lounge, not an empty box and not a dining row.

- Conversation zone: host sofa faces two guest chairs across a coffee table. Each occupied seat has a boom (or, at the console, a talkback) aimed at mouth height.
- Control zone: mixing desk with faders, meters, and a waveform screen. The engineer faces the talent. Two monitor cabinets toe in toward that chair.
- Back wall: bookcase plus wall shelves over a record credenza, with a banker's lamp on the credenza.
- Left wall: irregular acoustic panels, a corner trap, and an ON AIR plate. A wood-slat diffuser breaks the back wall between the set and the shelves.

Seat yaw comes from `lookAt` in `layout.json` (`orient.js`). Sofa and bookcase treat local −Z as forward; chairs, the monitor, and the speakers treat local +Z as forward.

See `refs/GAP.md` for what is procedural.
