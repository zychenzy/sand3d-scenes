# Indoor conservatory

A working glasshouse, not a ring of identical pots.

## Composition

Room 9.4 m × 7.6 m. The barrel vault springs at 2.36 m and crowns at about 3.98 m. Back and left sides are glazed above a brick knee. The front and the right side are the cutaway: ribs and a right-hand colonnade stay, the glass does not.

Circulation is a stone path just right of centre, from the open front to the potting corner. An island bed sits left of the path. A side gap between the island and the left bed leads to the nook.

### Raised beds

- Back bed: palm, ferns, a flower spike, and a climber on a trellis against the gable.
- Left bed: a low big-leaf plant, a fern, and a climber on the mullions.
- Island bed (focal): a branching canopy tree, a lower multi-stem shrub, ferns, and blossom spikes.

### Potting bench

Back-right, surface at 0.93 m. Plank top, legs, slat shelf, three drawers, splash, pegs, soil tray, trowel, seed packets. A galvanized can and a hose sit on the floor. Catalog pieces on and above the bench: two differently scaled pots with cuttings, a brush pot, jars, a mug, a banker’s lamp (emissive tube), a wall shelf with a thermos, and a round stool in front of the bench. A larger pot with a fern stands off the path.

### Sitting nook

Front-left. A backless museum bench with a cushion faces a round tea table. The dining chair is on the path side of the table. Its model faces local +Z; `rotationY` of −π/2 turns that face toward −X, into the table and the bench. Mugs and a jar with a sprig sit on the table. A pendant with an emissive bulb hangs under the low part of the vault.

## Light

Warm key from the open front-right casts hard shadows through the ribs and the canopy. A leaf-cookie spotlight mottles the island and the path. The pendant and the bench lamp are small warm pools. Unlit mode keeps the same colors without those lights.
