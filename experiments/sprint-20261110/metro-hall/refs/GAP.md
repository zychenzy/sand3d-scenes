# Gaps — North Quay concourse

The hall is procedural architecture plus three reused GLBs. There is no Objaverse ticket gate, escalator, or metro column in the repo, and copying bulk GLBs into this folder is out of bounds.

## Used

- Museum bench as a backless concourse perch (three, not a row). Sides face the map, the gate line, or the departures TV.
- Cafe flowerpot as a planter at each map column.
- KTV TV as a freestanding concourse monitor on a plinth. Its screen is a dark plastic panel, so the readable departures board is a separate hanging sign.

## Missing

- Gates, stairs, columns, machines, bins, and the route map are built from meshes, not scanned furniture.
- The bench has no backrest, so "facing" is which long side points at the target.
- No people, trains, escalators, or platform tunnel beyond the first flight.
- The route map is an original fictional network, not a city diagram.
- Contact darkening is a radial blob plus the directional shadow map, not a baked AO pass.
- Serving this folder alone will not resolve `../../../museum/...`. Launch from the repo root (see README).
