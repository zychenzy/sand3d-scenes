# Gaps

- No catalog GLB for a jeweler's bench, bench pin, vise, safe, rolling mill, or flex-shaft. Those are procedural and read as stylized solids, not scanned tools.
- Display bookcase and plinth bakes shipped without materials. They are tinted here (walnut case, limestone plinth).
- Jam jars stand in for rouge and pickle pots. They are scaled up so the gold lid reads.
- Rings, busts, and gems are deliberately large. Real millimeter jewelry would disappear in the toon room.
- Gem-case shelf heights are taken from the GLB bounds. If a tray clips a shelf, nudge `caseTrays` in `jewelry.js`.
- The library book-stand bake was tried as a catalog lectern and removed: it read as a dark podium in the salon. Orders sit on the consultation table instead.
- Banker's lamp is a library lamp. The shade is marked emissive so it reads as a task light.
- Contact shadows are discs, not a screen-space AO pass. The vendored toon path has no GTAO.
