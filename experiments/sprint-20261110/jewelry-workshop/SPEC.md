# Jeweler atelier

One cutaway room, two zones, one palette.

## Zones

- **Atelier** (back wall, wood floor). Making bench with pin, vise, and three drawers. Setting bench with a buffing motor. Materials chest (AU, AG, GEM, WAX) and a balance scale between them. Toolboards over each bench. Safe on the left. Stock cabinet on the right. A leather runner marks the aisle.
- **Salon** (front, burgundy rug). Glass display counter with rings, a necklace bust, and bracelets. Two client chairs face the counter; the jeweler's chair faces them from the aisle. Consultation table with two facing chairs. Stone plinth with a second bust. Shop window on the left wall.

## Facing

Dining-chair front is +Z at rotation 0 (same axis as the library room).

- Bench chairs and client chairs and the north salon chair use rotation π so they face −Z, toward the bench, counter, or table.
- The jeweler's chair and the south salon chair use rotation 0 so they face +Z into the work.
- Banker's lamps use rotation π so the shade reaches over the bench pin.
- Gem case and wall shelf use −π/2 so their fronts open toward +X.

## Light

Hard stepped toon, one key, one fill, and small warm pools at the bench lamps, pendants, and the counter. Bulb and shade meshes are marked emissive. Contact discs sit under furniture. No bloom, no outline.

## Palette

Walnut, brass, plaster, emerald velvet, burgundy rug, gold and silver pieces. Jewelry is oversized on purpose so it reads at room scale.
