# GAP — textile studio

The Objaverse bakes in this repo have chairs, tables, lamps, and a bench. They do not have a loom, sewing machine, bolt, spool, or cutting mat.

Built procedurally in `textile.js`:

- Four-shaft floor loom (warp, two shafts, reed, four treadles, cloth roll) and a rigid-heddle loom on a stand.
- Two domestic sewing machines on work tables.
- Trestle cutting table with mat, cloth, pattern, shears, ruler, weights.
- Horizontal bolt rack, upright bolt bin, floor spool rack, wall spool rack, cone creel.
- Hanging sample wall, folded chest, ironing board, warping board, aprons.

Reused without copying GLBs: dining chairs, square notions table, piano bench, museum bench, pendants, library lamps, flowerpot, mug.

Still approximate:

- Looms and machines are toon constructions, not scanned tools. Heddles are bars, not tied eyes.
- Contact ellipses stand in for AO. One hard shadow map (the front-right key). Window panes are emissive cards, not glass.
- Cloth patterns are generated canvas textures, not woven scans.
- The cutting table is seated height so the shared dining chair fits; it is not a 0.9 m standing industrial table.
- Library-lamp shades face as the baked mesh allows; a small emissive bulb is added at the shade.
