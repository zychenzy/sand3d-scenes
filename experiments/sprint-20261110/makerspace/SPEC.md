# Maker workshop — brief

One cutaway room, about 9.6 m by 7.6 m, 3.05 m high. Open on the front (+Z) and the right (+X).

## Zones

- **Tool wall** (back, left of center). Plywood backer, pegboard with three tool clusters, standing bench at 0.90 m with a vise, stool tucked to the bench, orange tool chest at the bench’s right end.
- **Electronics** (left wall). Two catalog study desks turned to the wall. Chairs and monitor faces point at the sitter. ESD mats, a meter, a soldering setup, task lamps.
- **Parts and printer** (back-right). Open bin rack with a missing bin, jars and spare spools on top, enclosed-frame printer on a cabinet with a lit bed, gantry, and one mounted spool.
- **Assembly** (center, the focal point). Scaled square table, four chairs facing in, one pulled out. Two pendants. A chassis and hardware on a cutting mat. A low finishing cart with prints sits between the table and the printer.

Clerestory on the left wall, steel joists over the back half only, shop tubes over the bench and the printer, contact blobs under furniture.

## Scale

Bench top 0.90 m. Seated desks and the assembly table 0.75–0.76 m. Chairs 0.86 m overall. Printer cabinet plus frame about 1.25 m. Aisles between the electronics chairs and the assembly chairs stay wide enough to walk.

## Acceptance

Pegboard reads immediately. Electronics chairs face the screens. Printer and bins read as a second zone, not a second copy of the table. Assembly chairs face the table. Lamps and the printer bed stay bright in toon and in unlit.
