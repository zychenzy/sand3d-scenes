# Gaps

The catalog has no boat, quay, or harbour mesh that fitted this site. Those parts are procedural.

Still missing, and left missing:

- Scanned or catalog hulls. Both boats are lofted sections with vertex colors, not surveyed hulls.
- The public bench is the museum oak-and-steel bench, not a cast-iron seafront seat. The cafe chairs are the indoor dining chairs from the cafe kit.
- Water has no reflection and no refraction. Depth is a vertex-color gradient, the surface is a low swell, and a foam strip marks the walls.
- Rigging is a mast, a boom, and a furled sail. Stays were left off: a line that thin aliases at the default camera.
- No people, no registration numbers, no fishing gear inside the boats beyond a crate, thwarts, and oars.
- The net is a repeating alpha grid on a sagging sheet, plus a folded bundle. It is not a photographed net.
- The village is the shed, the cafe pocket, the lane, and the bank. There is no street of houses.
- The east shore and the headland are simple ground, not a surveyed coast.
- Tide is an authored mid tide. The Crail photograph shows more wall than this.
