# Sources

Photos were viewed for the quay, the boats, and the working gear. They are not copied into git. The scene is drawn from them, not a reconstruction of one harbour.

## Fishing boats in Mevagissey harbour

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fishing_boats_in_Mevagissey_harbour.jpg
- https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/91/Fishing_boats_in_Mevagissey_harbour.jpg
- Nilfanion, 1 July 2011, 17:28. Camera 50° 16′ 06.8″ N, 4° 47′ 00.2″ W. CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported.
- Three different fishing boats against a stone wall: wheelhouses, a mast, orange fenders, calm reflective water, a low headland. Used for hull variety, fenders, and how a boat sits on the wall.

## Crail Harbour, Fife

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crail_Harbour,_Fife,_Scotland.JPG
- Kim Traynor, 7 June 2013, 15:59. CC BY-SA 3.0 (`{{self|cc-by-sa-3.0}}` on the file page).
- Inner stone basin, two unlike boats tucked to the wall, ladders, a step up to the street, green-grey water. Used for the L of wall and water, the ladders, and the level change. The photo is a lower tide than the scene: this quay keeps about 1.2 m of wall above the water so the boats still float and the ladders still reach.

## Nets on the Fish Quay, Mevagissey

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nets_on_the_Fish_Quay_Mevagissey_-_geograph.org.uk_-_2774375.jpg
- https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/2774375
- Peter Skynner, Geograph Britain and Ireland, 28 May 2007, 11:32. Camera 50° 16′ 11.4″ N, 4° 47′ 12″ W. CC BY-SA 2.0.
- A heap of manila and coloured rope with a fine pale net, not a tidy grid. Used for the west-pier work zone. This is the late-morning light the scene is lit for. The Mevagissey boat photo is late afternoon and the Crail photo is mid-afternoon.

Water in both harbour photos is green-grey in the basin. The scene uses that, darker offshore, with a pale foam line on the stone. No reference photograph is stored in the repo.
