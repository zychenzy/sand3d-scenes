# Small harbour quay

Working L-shaped stone quay, mid tide, clear late morning. The basin opens toward the east and the south. Water is y = 0. The deck is 1.22 m, the cafe terrace 1.70 m, the lane behind 2.05 m.

- Boat A: 7.4 m inshore boat with a wheelhouse, bow east, alongside the north wall.
- Boat B: 5.1 m clinker day boat with a low cuddy, bow south, alongside the west pier.
- West pier end holds the net rail, rope coils, and buoys. The fish shed opens onto the north quay. The cafe sits on the raised terrace with a bench on the deck below.
- Ladders drop to the water on the north wall, the west wall, and the pier end. A short slip leaves the north wall between the boats.

Cameras, lights, and furniture poses are in `layout.json`. Hulls, quay, water, shed, and cafe shell are procedural in `geometry.mjs`.
