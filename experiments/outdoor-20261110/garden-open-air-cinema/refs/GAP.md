# Gaps

What the references show, and what this scene does not claim to match.

- No deckchair, cart, tensile-screen, or projector-booth GLB exists in the local sand3d catalog. Those pieces are procedural. The reused GLBs are the cafe cup, thermos, jam jar, and empty flowerpot.
- Sun Pictures seats are linked sling benches, not separate X-frame deckchairs. The X-frame is a reading of the folding chairs in the brief, not a copy of that bench.
- The Sun Pictures screen shows a copyrighted film. The canvas here is generated in code and has no still, title, or face.
- Marburg uses colored washes and a site-specific gothic arch. Neither is in this garden. The key is one warm dusk direction plus fill, not crossed beams or spotlight pools.
- The Athens photograph is a hard urban court with a side cafe. It informed the wall, the side aisle, and the planted edge. The booth, the cable cover, and the festoon are brief requirements and are not clearly present in the three photographs.
- The cable cover is a low timber lid along the right aisle. It is a constructed route, not a hero object, and it is easy to miss from the high default camera.
- The projector lens faces the screen, so the high cameras mostly see the booth roof and the short snout. The close camera is aimed at the chairs, not into the booth.
- Plants are stylized toon silhouettes (forked trunks, flattened canopy layers, a cypress, shrubs, lavender, grass). They are not scanned foliage, and the outside trees are still simple.
- The lawn is a flat terrace with a raised path and beds, not a surveyed site. There is no water.
- Pots are grey empty bakes; the leaves in them are added locally.
