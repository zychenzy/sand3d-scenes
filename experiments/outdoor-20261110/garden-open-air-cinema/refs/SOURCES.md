# Photo references

Three public photographs, viewed for composition, proportions, and materials. None of the files are stored in this repository. Nothing from the pictures on the screens, posters, or faces was copied.

1. **Sun Picture Gardens, Broome, at dusk**
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_crowd_settle_in_at_dusk_to_watch_a_film_at_Sun_Picture_Gardens,_Broome,_WA,_Australia_02.jpg
   Ridiculopathy, 2024. CC0.
   Used for the dusk audience, deckchairs aimed at a screen, a central aisle, and trees around an open garden cinema.
   The screen in the photograph shows a commercial film. This scene uses an original abstract canvas instead.

2. **Open-air cinema, Athens (Anesis)**
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Open_air_movie_theater_at_Athens.jpg
   Badseed, 2008. CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL.
   Used for a walled court, chairs facing a screen on the wall, a side aisle, and a planted edge.
   Poster lettering and faces in that photograph were not copied.

3. **Marburg Open-Air-Kino, 2003**
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marburg-Open_Air-Kino_(2003).jpg
   Manfred & Barbara Aulbach, 2003. CC BY-SA 3.0.
   Used for a dusk framed screen, trees, and an audience in a garden court.
   The colored spotlight beams and the gothic arch in that photograph were not copied.

Rejected after viewing, and not used: the Augarten "Kino wie noch nie" gate installation (not an auditorium), the Akti Cinema night facade (posters and neon), and an inflatable AIRSCREEN (a different screen type). This scene uses a mast-and-cable tensile frame.
