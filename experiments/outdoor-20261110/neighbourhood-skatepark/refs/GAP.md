# GAP — neighbourhood skatepark

This is a compact composite, not a survey of one park.

Closed in the scene:

- Shallow bowl and quarterpipe are circular transitions (see `tests/results.txt`), with coping and a rolled mouth so the bowl meets the flat.
- Manual pad, steel ledge, stairs, and a round rail sit off the gate-to-quarter lane.
- Fence, open gate, path, benches, and trees are outside the riding lines.

Still open:

- From the high default camera the quarter's curve is carried by four toon bands, so the face reads softer than it does in the corner and close views.
- Chain-link is posts, rails, and vertical wires, not a woven mesh.
- Trees are built trunks and foliage clumps, not scanned crowns.
- Spectator seats are the existing sand3d bench (backless), not a bolted park bench.
- No skaters, bikes, or graffiti. The IOR reference is covered in paint; this park stays bare concrete so the obstacles stay readable.
- Coping is a round tube. Pool tile and steel channel are not modelled.
- Hard shadows on the fence stay a bit jagged at this map size.
