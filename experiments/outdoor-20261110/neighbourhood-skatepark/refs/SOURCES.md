# SOURCES — neighbourhood skatepark

Photos were used for proportions and materials. They are not copied into git.

## Lyon, shallow plaza bowl

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lyon_7e_-_Bowl_de_la_Guilloti%C3%A8re_(sept_2022).jpg

CC BY-SA 4.0, Sebleouf, 9 September 2022, 18:09. Guillotière skate bowl on the Rhône.

Used for the shallow oval sunk flush into a flat deck, a continuous coping line with the riding surface at one height, late-day light, and trees and people kept off the concrete.

## Macon, Central City bowl

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Macon_Skatepark_Bowl.jpg

CC BY-SA 4.0, Zoogybear, 1 July 2019.

Used for the painted steel coping (the reddish lip), smooth concrete transitions into a flat bottom, and a deck you can stand on behind the coping.

## Bucharest, IOR street section

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ledges,_stairs_with_rail_and_a_bank_of_the_IOR_Skatepark_in_Bucharest_(01).jpg

CC0, Neoclassicism Enthusiast.

Used for the concrete ledge with a steel angle on the arris, the round handrail on posts beside a short stair, and the open flat in front of the obstacle.

## Also consulted

- La Junta, Colorado, public domain (US National Archives): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Santa_Fe_Trail_-_La_Junta_Skatepark_-_NARA_-_7721575.jpg — grass edge, houses behind the concrete, flowing curves, long afternoon shadows.
- Shearwater, Nova Scotia, CC BY-SA 4.0, T.Newfeels: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shearwater_Skatepark_full_view.jpg — a small fenced pad with obstacles spaced so a lane of concrete stays open. The low wedge banks in that photo were not copied.
