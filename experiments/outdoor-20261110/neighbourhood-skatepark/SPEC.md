# Neighbourhood skatepark

Late-afternoon municipal concrete park, about 17 m by 12 m of riding surface inside a chain-link fence.

- Shallow oval bowl, 0.92 m deep, 63° wall, round coping, one eased mouth onto the flat.
- Quarterpipe, 1.34 m high, 77° circular transition (radius 1.74 m), deck and coping, shoulders rolled down to the flat.
- Street cluster off the east run-up: 0.18 m manual pad, 0.38 m ledge with a steel angle, three 0.15 m stairs, 23 mm round rail parallel to the treads.
- Gate on the south fence opens onto that run-up (about 9 m of clear flat).
- Spectator benches and mixed trees outside the fence; houses and a low rise behind.

See `layout.json` for cameras and lights, `geometry.js` for the ride-surface numbers.
