# Riverside tea pavilion

One outdoor scene. Metres. +Z is south (toward the path and the default camera). −Z is the river.

The hall is an open single-eave timber pavilion, not a temple axis and not a double-eave hall. The default camera sits south of the deck and looks under the front eave; the river stays visible under the back eave. A side creek on the west carries one stone arch. Seating is three groups (hall, bay, bank) plus one chair facing the water. The counter faces the hall.

Shading defaults to a four-step toon gradient with a warm key, a warm fill, and a cool fill from the river. Only the key casts shadows. `U` or `?mode=unlit` switches to flat color with the same maps and no shadows.

Poses live in `layout.json`. `pavilion.js` builds the terrain, hall, furniture, and plants. Reused GLBs are symlinks under `assets/`.
