# Sources

Public photographs used for composition, proportions, and materials. Reference images are not stored in git.

- West Lake pavilion and water, Hangzhou, 29 May 2012, Daniel Vorndran / DXR, CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:West_Lake_Pavilion_and_Water_Lilies,_Hangzhou_120529_1.jpg
  Open tiled pavilion, grey-green water, haze, willows at the side. The double eave and the lotus field are not copied. This scene uses one eave so the seating stays visible.

- Teahouse in People's Park, Chengdu, 25 Sep 2015, Daderot, CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teahouse_in_Peoples_Park_-_Chengdu,_China_-_DSC05348.jpg
  Open single-eave halls, dark timber, grouped bamboo chairs, a stone path, and modest upturned eaves that leave the interior readable. The inscribed gate is not the focal point.

- Teahouse at Wangjianglou Park, Chengdu, Daderot, CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teahouse_-_Wangjianglou_Park_-_Chengdu,_China_-_DSC05753.jpg
  Bamboo grove, clustered tables, clear paths, white cups. Plastic chairs, umbrellas, and the red hall are not copied.

- Rocks and water, Wangjianglou Park, Daderot, CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rocks_and_water_-_Wangjianglou_Park_-_Chengdu,_China_-_DSC05739.jpg
  Irregular rock and grass edge, a scholar rock, and a shallow margin.

- Tea in People's Park, Chengdu, Daderot, CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tea_in_People%27s_Park_-_Chengdu,_China_-_DSC05362.jpg
  White gaiwan, wood table, green tea. Used for the close table setting.

A Jixian pavilion sunrise frame was looked at and rejected: the contrast is a silhouette, and the double eave and sun disc do not fit this morning hall.
