# Gaps

The sand3d catalog has no pavilion, willow, bamboo chair, stone bridge, or gaiwan. Those are built in `pavilion.js`. The flowerpot and thermos are the only reused GLBs.

Still approximate:

- The roof is a continuous hip with a course texture, ridge roll, and hip rolls. It is not a field of individual pantiles.
- Willow and bamboo leaves are flat cards on twigs. They read as curtains, not scanned foliage.
- Water is a tinted surface with a ripple texture. It does not flow or reflect.
- There are no people.
- The long-spout kettle is a Chengdu service shape, not a measurement from these frames.
- The bridge is one arch over the side creek. The far approach is not a destination.
- Mist is distance fog and paler far trees, not a volume.
- The menu board uses rule lines. It does not fake Chinese characters.
- The lowest terrace tread sits near the water line, so the default frame shows the upper treads more clearly than the last riser.
