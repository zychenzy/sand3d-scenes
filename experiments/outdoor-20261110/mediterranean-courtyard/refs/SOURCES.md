# SOURCES — mediterranean-courtyard

Composition references. No reference images are stored in git. Each page was fetched and the picture was used for proportions and materials, not traced.

## Used

1. [Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos, inner courtyard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alcazar_of_the_Christian_Monarchs_-_Inner_Courtyard.jpg) — Greenshed, 2008-01-10, public domain. Open court, white arcade along two edges, tile roofs, orange trees, stone pool terrace. Used for the L of one-storey arcades, open sky, and barrel tile roofs. This court is one olive and one potted citrus, not an orange grove, and the fountain is a raised tiled tank rather than a long pool.

2. [Patio de los Naranjos, Palacio de Viana](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:One_of_the_twelve_patios_in_the_Palacio_de_Viana_(27651290095).jpg) — Bobo Boom, 2015-09-15, CC BY 2.0. Raised tiled basin, pots on the coping, brick paving against a pale gallery, a blue window, a vine, warm afternoon shadows. Used for the fountain, coping pots, brick-versus-limestone terraces, the blue shutter, and the light direction.

3. [Casa de los Hoces, patio y fuente](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Casa_de_los_Hoces,_patio_y_fuente.jpg) — CFA1877, 2025-08-28, CC BY-SA 4.0. Brick arches on plaster piers around a round stone basin. Used for the arch profile and the plaster-pier / brick-trim contrast. The basin here is rectangular.

## Consulted, not copied

4. [Patio Cordobés](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Patio_Cordobes_(27726688459).jpg) — Jocelyn Erskine-Kellie, 2017-10-14, CC BY-SA 2.0, Barrio San Basilio. Terracotta pots and geraniums. The hanging-pot wall is denser than this court; only the pot and flower color carried over.

5. [Patio dans la Judería](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Patio_dans_la_Juderia_(8281510681).jpg) — Nicolas Vollmer, 2012-09-07, CC BY 2.0. A roofed restaurant court with a fountain and many tables. Furniture idea only. This court is not roofed, and it has three tables rather than a dining grid.
