# GAP — mediterranean-courtyard

No single photograph has this whole program: an open arcade, a tiled fountain, an olive, bougainvillea, and a few cafe tables. The three sources in `SOURCES.md` were combined. The two consulted patios were not rebuilt.

## What the photos have that this scene does not

- The Alcázar court is a monumental orange grove with a long pool. Here there is one olive and one citrus in an urn.
- Viana and Hoces use pebble rings and, at Hoces, a round basin. The court is running-bond clay tile with limestone gallery slabs. It is not a pebble mosaic and not a strict herringbone.
- Patio Cordobés is a wall of hanging pots. This court has a handful of pots and two coping urns.
- The Judería patio is indoors under a roof, with many tables. Tables here are a small overlay: two in the galleries, one under the olive.
- The upper floor is only the southwest house block and one blue window, not a full two-storey palace wall.
- Catalog chairs are modern painted-metal dining chairs. Tables are small marble rounds. Flowerpots were gray plastic and are tinted terracotta. Foliage, the olive, and the bougainvillea are toon masses, not scanned plants.
