# Woodland picnic grove

A small shaded picnic grove on the east bank of a rocky creek. The social focus is three staggered picnic tables. The timber footbridge is the way across. A charcoal grill and timber prep counter sit uphill of the middle table. A two-board trail sign marks the split between the creek trail and the grove. One fallen log faces the water; another faces the far table.

Units are metres. Table tops are 0.76 m, seats 0.45 m, on galvanized frames with plank tops, matching the Look Rock picnic tables rather than a timber A-frame. The bridge is a plank deck on log stringers with round-post rails, wider than the Cosby Creek footlog so a picnic path can cross. Afternoon light is one directional sun from the west, high enough that canopy shadows fall as patches. Fill and the hemisphere do not cast shadows.

Cameras: default overview, `corner` from the west bank through the bridge, `close` on the near table. Orbit stays above the ground.
