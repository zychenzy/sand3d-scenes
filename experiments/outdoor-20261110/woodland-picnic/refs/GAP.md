# GAP — woodland picnic

What the references show, and what this grove changes or does not have.

## Closed in the scene

- Rocky creek with a deeper olive center, a paler edge, a short drop, and white riffle pieces. Not a flat rectangle.
- Rounded boulders in the channel and on the banks, some with moss caps.
- Three picnic tables with plank tops, plank benches, and splayed metal frames, spaced along a gravel path instead of in a row.
- Charcoal grill: a rectangular box on a single post, lid ajar, set a few metres off the nearest table.
- Timber bridge on log stringers with round posts and rails, stepped onto the banks.
- Two-board wooden trail sign at the path split.
- Fallen log on the east bank facing the water, and a shorter log facing the far table.
- Mixed hardwoods (broad, tall, split, leaning) plus a few hemlocks, rhododendron-scale shrubs, and ferns.
- One west sun for shadows. Fill does not cast, so shadows do not cross.

## Honest differences

- Chimneys Picnic Area has 68 sites along the West Prong of the Little Pigeon River, which is wider and more of a river than this creek. The scene is a three-table grove, and the channel is closer to Cosby Creek's width than to the West Prong.
- The Cosby photo is a late-winter footlog, narrower than a metre, with the rail mainly on one side and a broken far post. This bridge is about 1.0 m wide with rails on both sides so the path can cross. Summer foliage is filled in.
- Look Rock tables sit on a grassy ridge beside a road. Their frame and grill are used here; the road and mown grass are not. The streamside shade follows Chimneys.
- The Baxter Creek sign was photographed in snow, with routed mileage. These boards are painted summer wood and only name Creek Trail and the picnic grove.
- The timber prep counter, cutting board, charcoal bucket, and small trash can are not in the Look Rock grill photo. Chimneys lists grills and trash receptacles; the counter is the brief's prep point and is schematic.
- No sand3d catalog mesh matched a picnic table, grill, bridge, tree, or sign. Those are built in `woodland.js`. The mug, thermos, and jam jar are the cafe bakes, placed on the tabletops.
- Canopies are flattened icosahedron clumps on branched trunks, not leaf cards or scans. Conifers are stacked cones.
- Water does not animate, reflect, or refract. Foam is a few pale chips on the surface.
- The trash can is a plain cylinder, not a measured bear-proof bin.
- Dapple comes from canopy masses blocking one sun. It will not match a photograph of hundreds of small leaf shadows.
