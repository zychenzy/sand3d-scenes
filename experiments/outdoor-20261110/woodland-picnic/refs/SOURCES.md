# SOURCES — woodland picnic

Photos below were opened and used for composition, proportions, and materials. They are not copied into git.

## Chimneys Picnic Area, Great Smoky Mountains

Streamside shade, boulder creek, and the amenity list (picnic table, grill, benches).

- Place page: https://www.nps.gov/places/chimneys-picnic-area.htm
  Heavily forested, consistent shade, picnic tables and grills, seating. Elevation about 835 m. The full area has 68 sites; this scene is a three-table excerpt.
- Small cascade in the river at Chimneys Picnic Area, NPS photo, 16 July 2010, public domain:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Small_cascade_in_river_at_Chimneys_Picnic_Area_(291d14fb-1dd8-b71c-0750-5bb0c39a671a).jpg
  Used for the green pool, white drop over a rock lip, rounded gray boulders, and the layered hardwood bank.
- Same river as the picnic backdrop, NPS NPGallery:
  https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/9ED75C67-1DD8-B71C-0759-804DFA29E14B

## Cosby Creek footbridge, Great Smoky Mountains

- Brian Stansberry, 29 March 2009, CC BY 3.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cosby-creek-footbridge-gsmnp1.jpg
  Narrow log deck, round posts, a single top rail, boulder abutments, mossy rocks, and a trunk lying in the creek. The scene bridge keeps that timber construction and adds a second rail and a wider plank deck (see GAP).

## Look Rock Picnic Area, Great Smoky Mountains

Table and grill construction. These sites are a wooded ridge with a road, not a creek.

- Picnic table and charcoal grill, NPS / NPGallery, public domain:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Picnic_Table_and_Grill_at_Look_Rock_Picnic_Area_(8fe649f8-7d7b-44aa-9394-49decdb3b1da).jpg
  Plank top, single plank benches, splayed galvanized tube frame, rectangular grill box on one post, set off from the table.
- Staggered tables along a gravel path under mixed hardwoods:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Picnic_Tables_at_Look_Rock_Picnic_Area_(227fdd65-023f-48e4-b309-e875d6fc3c8f).jpg
- Grill alone, box on a post, canopy shade:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grill_at_Look_Rock_Picnic_Area_(3fab2dfb-8770-4671-8d0d-592569a37bb0).jpg

## Baxter Creek trail sign, Mount Sterling, Great Smoky Mountains

- Brian Stansberry, CC BY 3.0. Wooden post, two stacked weathered boards, dark lettering, arrows:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Baxter-creek-signpost-mt-sterling-nc1.jpg
  The scene boards are summer-colored and name this grove's paths; they are not a copy of the snowy mileage text.
