# Sources — sculpture park

Photo references are for landscape composition, proportion, and material tone. The sculptures in this scene are original forms. None of the cited works is reproduced.

## 1. Storm King Art Center, central prairie

- File: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Storm_King_sculpture_area.jpg
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8d/Storm_King_sculpture_area.jpg
- Author: Ironandwire, 10 October 2019, own photograph
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Camera: 41° 25′ 30.55″ N, 74° 03′ 32.94″ W, looking across the central prairie
- Used for: open meadow as the ground plane; sculptures standing in grass rather than on a plinth grid; unequal spacing and different silhouettes; foreground grass, a few specimen trees, and a darker tree band against the sky

## 2. Louisiana Museum of Modern Art, the field

- File: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Louisiana_Museum_Field.jpg
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Louisiana_Museum_Field.jpg
- Author: Leonardo Pilara, 1 August 2013 (Flickr, reviewed on Commons 7 January 2024)
- License: CC BY-SA 2.0
- Used for: lawn as the social ground; one dark sculptural mass in the middle distance; a curved seat turned toward the view; layered shrubs and trees at the edges; water as a calm horizontal band rather than a flat blue rectangle dropped in the grass

## 3. Olympic Sculpture Park, Seattle waterfront berm

- File: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Olympic_Sculpture_Park_Seattle_seen_from_waterfront.JPG
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1f/Olympic_Sculpture_Park_Seattle_seen_from_waterfront.JPG
- Author: MarmadukePercy, 5 June 2010, own photograph
- License: CC BY-SA 3.0
- Description on Commons: view of the berm along Seattle’s waterfront
- Used for: earth built as a landform (the meadow mounds), grass meeting a constructed edge, and individual trees set into grade instead of a repeated row of identical canopies
