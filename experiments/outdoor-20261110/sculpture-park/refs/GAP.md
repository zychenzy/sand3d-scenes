# Gaps — sculpture park

What the references do not contain, and what the scene does not claim to solve.

## Reference gaps

- The Storm King prairie photograph is bright sun with a blue sky. The brief asks for soft overcast. The photo was used for meadow structure and sculpture placement, not for the sunny key.
- The Louisiana photograph’s water is the Øresund, a horizon, not a garden basin. The curved seat in that photo faces the view; the basin, stone coping, and ripple read are designed for this scene and are not in the photo.
- The Olympic photograph is the waterfront berm wall, not the meadow with Calder’s Eagle. It supports the landform and “trees in grass” relationship only. It does not show a gravel loop.
- No public photo in this set is a close study of gravel texture or of a stone coping joint. Gravel is a vertex-color ribbon; coping is faceted stone segments.
- Wikimedia’s Spoonbridge and Cherry views were not used as a model. The focal ribbon is an original stroke, not a spoon, bridge, or cherry.

## Catalog gaps

- `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse` bakes in this repo are indoor props. There is no monumental sculpture, terrain, tree, or water mesh to place.
- The only reused GLB is the museum bench (oak seat, metal X-legs, target height 0.46 m). It reads as a path bench, not as a limestone garden seat. The curved seat facing the ribbon is procedural stone.
- Foliage is faceted toon masses: oak clusters, pine cones, birch stems, a willow of tubes. Not botanical scans.
- Water is an opaque colored surface with a darker projected trace of the ribbon, two-dimensional ripples, and a built coping. It is not a reflective shader.
- Mounds are hemispheres sunk slightly into the ground plane. They do not include mowing stripes, soil profiles, or a real path cross-section where the spur meets the knoll.
- The west coil and east fins are not sited works by a named artist. Ring seating and plate leans were tuned by eye in the toon renderer.
