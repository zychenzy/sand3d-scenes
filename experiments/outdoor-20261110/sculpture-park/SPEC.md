# Sculpture park — brief

A designed contemporary sculpture garden, not a field of cubes.

- One strong curved focal piece over a reflecting basin.
- Two supporting works, different material and different geometry: limestone rings on a mound, leaning steel plates in gravel.
- Unequal meadow mounds, a closed gravel loop, seats aimed at the works.
- Soft overcast daylight with tonal depth. Toon hard default, Unlit toggle.
- Human scale: bench seat 0.46 m, coping about 0.28 m, ribbon crest about 5.5 m.

Measurable poses, cameras, and the acceptance checks live in `layout.json`. Photo notes are in `refs/SOURCES.md` and `refs/GAP.md`.
