# Community allotment

One outdoor plot, metres, toon-hard shading with an unlit toggle.

## Site

- Fence about 15 m by 13 m. Close-board along the north side, post-and-wire on the other three. The south gate parks open clear of the chip spine.
- Beds are narrow enough to work from the path (reach about 0.55–1.85 m). Soil sits below the timber rim.
- Paths: spine through the gate, a cross path, headlands, and a gravel apron at the shed. Nothing decorative stands in them.

## Crops

Nine beds, different silhouettes and stages: mixed-height bean canes with bare tips and chard on the path lip, mature chard, mixed lettuce, heading brassicas, fruiting tomatoes with a squash at the south end, standing leeks, summer raspberry canes, cotyledon seedlings, and mid-stage roots.

## Working corner and picnic

- Shed on the north-west apron, mono-pitch roof falling east into a ribbed water butt. Two compost bays and a south-facing potting bench with three pots and a seed tray. Door number 4.
- Picnic table on the south gravel pad. Benches face the top. A dining chair at each head faces the table. A mug and a bowl of tomatoes sit on the top.
- Cold frame, leaning tools, a can, and a hose stay off the paths.

## Light and cameras

One morning key from the south-east. Hemisphere and a cool fill do not cast. Default view fits the plot. Corner is outside the fence. Close stands on the mid path, looking along the bean row.
