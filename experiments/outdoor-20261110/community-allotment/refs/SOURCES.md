# Sources

Photographs looked at while laying out this plot. Reference files are not in git.

## Used

1. [Allotment sheds and spring sunshine](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Allotment_sheds_and_spring_sunshine_-_geograph.org.uk_-_3356529.jpg) — John Sutton, 4 March 2013, Cambridge. CC BY-SA 2.0. Also [geograph 3356529](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/3356529).
   Taken: a small boarded shed with a corrugated roof, a black water butt, pallet compost beside the shed, a grass path, low morning sun, and ordinary houses behind the plot.
   Left out: the scrap sheet, ladder, and junk piled against the shed.

2. [Raised vegetable bed at Boreham, Essex](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raised_vegetable_bed_at_Boreham,_Essex,_England.jpg) — Acabashi, 11 June 2017. CC BY-SA 4.0.
   Taken: a timber raised bed with mixed lettuce sizes and colours, soil still visible, and a path hard against the rim.

3. [Runner bean border, Easton Lodge Gardens](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Runner_bean_border_Easton_Lodge_Gardens_walled_garden_Little_Easton_Essex_England_01.jpg) — Acabashi, 15 June 2025. CC BY-SA 4.0.
   Taken: cane A-frames with bare tips, vines at different heights, chard along the near edge, and a path beside the row. A water butt sits further back in that photo.
   The photograph is a walled garden. This plot uses an allotment fence, not that brick wall.
