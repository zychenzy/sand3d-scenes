# Outdoor amphitheatre

A small performance bowl in a sloped lawn, warm evening before the show.

- Curved stone seat tiers, not a chair grid. Every tier faces the timber stage.
- Side access stairs outside the arc, plus a short stair onto stage right.
- Backstage storage shed on the stage-right edge, crates visible.
- Two lighting poles with lamp heads. No spotlight pools.
- Forecourt on the crest: one table with two chairs facing it, one bench facing the stage, clustered pots.
- Default camera shows the whole site. Corner crosses the stage toward the bowl. Close is the front tier and the deck.
