# Sources

Real public photographs used for the bowl, the stone tiers, the side stairs, and the evening timber stage. No reference images are stored in git.

1. **English Garden amphitheatre, Munich** — small grass bowl, continuous curved terraces, tree wall behind the open end.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:English_garden_amphitheatre.jpg
   N p holmes, 28 June 2008. CC BY-SA 3.0. The file page describes “the new amphitheatre in the English Garden, Munich – a small open air theatre created in 1985.”

2. **Tamar Park amphitheatre, Admiralty, Hong Kong** — wide stone seat tiers, a side stair, and a low outdoor stage with a planted backdrop.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_Admiralty_%E6%B7%BB%E9%A6%AC%E5%85%AC%E5%9C%92_Tamar_Park_s_Amphitheatre_outdoor_stone_seats_n_stairs_May-2013.JPG
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_Admiralty_Tamar_Park_s_Amphitheatre_outdoor_stage_May-2013.JPG
   Wikimedia Commons, May 2013 (category “May 2013 in Tamar Amphitheatre”).

3. **Regent’s Park Open Air Theatre, London** — warm evening before a performance, timber stage house, lighting towers, trees behind the bowl.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Regents_Park_Open_Air_Theatre_-_2.jpg
   Alan Stanton, 17 May 2014, 19:43. “A snap of the audience before the play began.” Flickr source linked from the file page.

Baked props are existing Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse GLBs, symlinked from `museum/`, `cafe/`, `cinema/`, and `restaurant/`. Credits are in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`. Three.js r170 is the vendored build under `cinema/vendor` (MIT).
