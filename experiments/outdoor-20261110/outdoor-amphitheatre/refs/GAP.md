# Gaps

The three references were opened and used for composition. None of them is this exact small timber stage plus stone bowl, so the scene is a scaled synthesis.

- **English Garden** is the bowl: four or five low grass arcs, no chair grid, trees closing the back. It has almost no built stage. The scene keeps that continuous arc and adds a modest timber stage in the open end.
- **Tamar Park** is the stone: thick pale seat blocks and a real side stair. Its stage is a low paved curve in front of a concrete ivy wall, not a timber deck, and the seats are civic granite rather than a grass slope. Seat blocks here are straight segments along the arc (about 0.8–1.1 m), which matches those slabs more than a smooth torus.
- **Regent’s Park** is the evening: warm sky, timber stage, lighting towers, trees. It is a large raked chair house. Those chairs were not copied. Poles here are short garden masts with lamp heads, not the fly towers in that photograph.
- No water. Union Terrace’s grass terraces were not used as a fourth reference; the side stairs are the Tamar / terrace idea, kept outside the arc so the bowl stays one curve.
- Catalog has no amphitheatre seat, stage, pole, tree, or crate. Those are procedural. Reused GLBs are only the forecourt bench, table, two chairs, flowerpots, and two stage speakers.
- Lamp heads are emissive meshes. There are no spotlights and no projected pools.
- The bench is a backless slab. Its long axis runs across the sightline so one side faces the stage. Chair and speaker facing uses the same yaw as the reviewed boardgame room: rotationY 0 looks toward +Z.
- Grass is a heightfield with stone caps on top. The vertical risers cover the steps; the field is not a smooth sculpted berm between every block.
- Foliage is low-poly masses (squashed spheres, cones, blades), varied by kind, not scanned trees.
- The dusk sky is a viewport gradient (blue above, warm at the bottom of the frame). It does not move as a physical dome when you orbit.
- Stage speakers are the shared indoor speaker bake. The cone is the model’s local +X, so their yaw is a quarter-turn off the chair convention. They toe in toward the bowl.
