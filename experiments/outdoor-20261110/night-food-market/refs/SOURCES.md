# Photo sources

Public reference photos used for composition, stall depth, awnings, service clutter, and night lighting. Image files are not stored in git.

1. Raohe Street Night Market, Songshan, Taipei — Felix Filnkoessl, 2 July 2017, CC BY-SA 2.0
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2017-07-02_Raohe_Street_Night_Market.jpg
   Used for the linear stall row, dark sky against warm stall light, shophouse wall behind the stands, and a pedestrian lane down the middle. The built scene is a short courtyard-length row, not the full street.

2. Beef-noodle booth, Raohe Street Night Market — 玄史生, 24 December 2013, CC BY-SA 3.0
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beef_Noodle_Soup_Booth_at_Raohe_Street_Night_Market_20131224.jpg
   Used for the fabric awning over an open counter, the price board (beef noodle, offal soup, dry noodles), stock pots, communal tables in front of the stall, and gas cylinders plus bins in the working zone beside the cook.

3. Nakasu yatai stalls, Hakata, Fukuoka — Yoshikazu TAKADA, 27 July 2015, CC BY 2.0
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nakasu_Yatai_Stalls_(19979437930).jpg
   Used for compact stall fronts, paper lanterns and bulb strings, counter depth, and a night exposure where the stall interior stays readable. Communal tables here are in the customer lane rather than counter stools only.

Asset programs are existing Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse bakes, symlinked. See `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
