# Attributions

Terrain, water, trees, tents, fire ring, log seats, picnic table, dock, canoe, and the lantern are procedural meshes in `campsite.js`. No external model for those.

Catalog meshes are symlinks to existing cafe bakes from Hugging Face `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse` (CC BY 4.0):

- **thermos** → `assets/thermos__c85ee7b8828b.glb` — "Thermos" by Platon_Debik, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/c85ee7b8828b44998bb1cec3971424ec, CC BY 4.0
- **cup_mug** → `assets/cup_mug__6da6f7f87b8d.glb` — "Stylized Coffee Cup" by n30hrtgdv, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6da6f7f87b8d4706a563331657d6b5c3, CC BY 4.0
- **jam_jar** → `assets/jam_jar__4e43eb29aa14.glb` — "jam jar" by jwpthecoolguy2, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/4e43eb29aa144bdbada1ec500d218a81, CC BY 4.0
- **bucket** (flowerpot mesh) → `assets/flowerpot__4a270f247734.glb` — "Flowerpot" by thespaceman, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/4a270f2477344b55b359e2913f5530f2, CC BY 4.0
