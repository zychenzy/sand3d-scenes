# GAP — lakeside campsite

No single public photo in the sources shows this whole program at once: two different tents, a stone fire ring, a picnic kit, and a timber landing, at blue hour. The scene is assembled from the three daylight references above.

Closed in this scene:

- Ridge tent and a lower dome tent, different silhouette and cloth color, doors toward the fire, poles, fly, and guys.
- Stone ring, short flame, and three seats whose fronts point at the fire. The glow is a small point light (about 0.6–0.7, falloff under 5 m).
- Picnic table with benches facing the tabletop, plus a grate, pot, cooler, and catalog thermos, mugs, jar, and bucket.
- Short plank landing with posts, a waterline stain, and a ramp up from the shore.
- Shoreline that wanders by about 2.3 m, with a wet band, sand, rocks, and reeds. Water is a meshed surface, paler at the edge and darker offshore.
- Pines, spruce, cedar, snags, and saplings at different heights and leans, plus low shrubs. Trunks cast contact shadows; branch meshes do not, so the ground is not a lattice of branch shadows.

Still open:

- The three photo references are daylight. The blue-hour sky, moon, and fire glow are authored, not matched to a dusk plate.
- Birkenhead shows the view from a dock, not the dock’s carpentry. Plank size, posts, and the ramp are built to a typical short landing, not traced from that frame.
- None of the references show a stone fire ring or a second tent. Those are built to the brief.
- Local sand3d bakes have no tent, tree, boat, or dock. Those pieces are procedural. Catalog meshes reused here are a thermos, two mugs, a jam jar, and a flowerpot used as a wash bucket.
- Foliage is toon cones on branches, not scanned needles. Crowns are layered and uneven, and they still read as a stylized forest.
- Water has geometric ripples and a depth color. It does not reflect the trees.
- The canoe is an extra shore prop so the landing has a boat. It is not in the three reference frames.
