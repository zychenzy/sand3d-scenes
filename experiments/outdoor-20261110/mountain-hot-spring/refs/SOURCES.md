# Sources

Three public photographs were used for the pool, the shelter, and the lantern. They were opened and inspected. No reference images are stored in git.

## Tsurunoyu Onsen rotenburo, Nyūtō, Akita

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tsurunoyu_onsen_rotenburo.JPG
- File: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/ca/Tsurunoyu_onsen_rotenburo.JPG
- Markmark28, 7 February 2004. CC BY-SA 3.0.

Used for the milky blue-green mineral water with adults chest-deep, the rounded river-stone coping, the dark timber bathhouse tight against the pool (deep eave, round post, window), the wooden bucket on the rim, light steam, and the mountain slope behind the bath.

## Shinhodaka Onsen, Miyama-sō rotenburo

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sin-Hodaka_spa_Miyama-sou_roten_2008.jpg
- Soica2001, 15 October 2008. Public domain (author released the file).

Used for the irregular stone outline, water clear enough that submerged boulders and the bottom stay visible, the pale mineral line where water meets rock, bank reeds, and the forested mountain behind the bath.

## Takanoyu Onsen rotenburo, Akita

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Takanoyu_Onsen_Rotenburo_003.JPG
- RickardA, 18 September 2007. CC0.

Used for the timber post-and-beam shelter at the bath, the hanging paper lantern under the eave, dark water that reads as a basin, the wood rail, and the forest view past the rim.

## Also looked at, not used as the model

- Azohara hot spring, rectangular plank tub and spillway: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Azohara_Hot_Spring.jpg
- Nigorigo Onsen, large ochre pool, metal-roof pavilion, bamboo spout: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Outdoor_hot_spring_bath_in_Nigorigo_Onsen.jpg

The rectangular tub and the metal roof were rejected. The spillway and the bamboo spout informed the overflow and the kakehi.
