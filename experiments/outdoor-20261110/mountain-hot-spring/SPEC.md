# Mountain hot spring

Blue-hour rotenburo on a mountain terrace.

- Irregular stone bathing pool, about 5 m by 4 m, with an underwater seat.
- Water surface below the coping; floor about 0.9 m down so the basin reads as deep.
- Timber washing and changing shelter open toward the pool.
- Reed clumps and a bamboo screen, with the mountain left visible.
- Stone overflow, a short plank bridge, and a dry deck facing the view.
- A few warm lanterns. Toon hard by default, unlit on U.

Measurable poses and cameras live in `layout.json`.
