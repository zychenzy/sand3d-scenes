# Rooftop garden

One outdoor room: a city roof terrace in warm low sun.

Foreground is the stair head, stone landing, and herb beds. Midground is stepped planters and a single dining table under a timber pergola, with chairs facing the top. Background is a one-step-down lounge, a low parapet and rail, and a skyline that dips in the center so the view stays open.

Toon hard is the default. Unlit is a real material swap, not a missing shader. See `layout.json` for metres, cameras, and the checks in `tests/layout.test.mjs`.
