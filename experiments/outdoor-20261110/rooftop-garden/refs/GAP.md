# Gaps — rooftop garden

The three photographs were opened and used. None of them is a single golden-hour frame of this whole program.

Not in the references:

- Sansome is midday blue sky and hard sun, not amber hour. 221 Main is night, with globe lights and a lit pergola that this scene deliberately does not copy. The Ashmolean frame has low sun and long shadows, but the sky is blown toward white. The peach sky and warm key light are extrapolated from that low sun, not copied from a golden-hour plate.
- No reference shows a stair head, a slot drain, or a scupper. The bulkhead, open door, downpipe, and grate are built so the roof reads as a roof. They are not traced from these photos.
- The references show large bowls (Sansome) and tall raised beds (221 Main), not three literal stair-stepped planter walls. The steps are the brief's beds, using the raised-bed height and the path-shaping of 221 Main.
- Ashmolean planting is a continuous grass hedge. A hedge across this parapet would block the skyline, so grasses and the olive stay on the side tiers. The view edge is a low upstand and a rail, closer to Sansome.
- No catalog mesh is an outdoor dining set, a timber pergola, or a city. The oak trestle table, wooden chairs, sofa, coffee table, bench, mugs, and pots are indoor bakes standing in for terrace furniture. Pergola, planters, herbs, stair, parapet, and skyline are procedural.
- Foliage is sculpted toon geometry (branched stems, flat leaf paddles, blades, spikes). It is not scanned plants.
- The sofa and bench keep their baked materials. They are not reupholstered outdoor fabrics.
- Drainage is a grate, scupper, and downpipe. There is no animated water.
