# Sources — rooftop garden

Public photographs used for composition, proportions, and materials. No reference images are stored in git.

## 1. 343 Sansome Street roof garden, San Francisco

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:343_Sansome_St._roof_garden,_SF_2.JPG

Photograph by BrokenSphere, 22 August 2008, CC BY-SA. Direct file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/343_Sansome_St._roof_garden,_SF_2.JPG

What it shows: an urban roof terrace in hard sun. Multi-stem trees grow out of large planters with irregular, leafy crowns and visible trunks, not balls on sticks. A slatted bench and loose chairs make a low social group separate from smaller tables. A low concrete parapet carries a metal rail, and the foreground changes from stone pavers to a finer deck. Adjacent towers sit behind the planting.

Used for: olive branching, planter scale, parapet-and-rail, a lounge seat that is not the dining set, and the stone landing at the stair.

## 2. 221 Main Street roof terrace, San Francisco

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:221_Main_Street_Terrace_-_Night_View_-_Entry_and_Trellis_Amenity.jpg

Photograph by Daniel Prostak, 16 April 2018, CC BY-SA as published on the file page. Direct file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/221_Main_Street_Terrace_-_Night_View_-_Entry_and_Trellis_Amenity.jpg

What it shows: a roof patio at night. A timber pergola with one layer of parallel rafters covers only part of the terrace. Raised linear planters bend a deck path. The city is a skyline behind and above the roof, not a wall across the foreground. Seating is off to one side of the planted walk.

Used for: pergola member sizes and one-way rafters, planters that shape the path, deck boards, and keeping towers at the sides of the view.

## 3. Ashmolean Museum rooftop dining terrace, Oxford

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ashmolean_Museum_Oxford_Rooftop_Dining_Room_Terrace_2014.jpg

Photograph by Sarah Casey, 9 September 2014, CC BY-SA as published on the file page. Direct file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Ashmolean_Museum_Oxford_Rooftop_Dining_Room_Terrace_2014.jpg

What it shows: timber deck boards, square tables with chairs pulled in to face the tops, and a band of tall grasses and spiky plants along the edge. Roofs of the city still read above that planting. The sun is low enough to throw long shadows across the boards; the sky in the photo is bright.

Used for: chairs facing one table, board direction, grasses as a soft edge rather than a hedge wall, and the warm low sun. This scene keeps a single table instead of the photo's grid of tables.
