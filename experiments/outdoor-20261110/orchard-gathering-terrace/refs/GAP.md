# Gaps

What the photographs do not cover, and what this scene still gets wrong.

- Altapass is a large commercial planting, late in the year, with hundreds of small similar trees. This place is a handful of fruiting trees at gathering scale. The photo supplied the slope and the bands of grass.
- The Malham walls are in pasture, and the trees behind them are bare. There is no orchard, table, or harvest gear in that frame. The entrance piers in the photo are a metal gate; here the opening is stone steps.
- No single photograph showed this combination of table, press, and shed. Those are assembled from the pergola, press, and barn pictures.
- The Cotswold barn is a threshing barn. The shed here is shorter and one room, with the same honey courses, clay-tile gable, vent slit, and a door facing the terrace. It is not a copy of that building.
- A wooden U-pick shelter (Mack's Apples) and industrial pallet-crate stacks (Loddington Farm) were looked at and not used. The shelter is timber, not stone. The crate stacks are warehouse pallets, not a few hand crates.
- Grape bunches are clumps of spheres on the rafters. They read as fruit from the default camera. They are not botanical clusters, and the canopy is thinner than a mature vine in August.
- The press is the Somerset screw-press silhouette: beam, two iron uprights, screw, basket of apples. The basket slats are boxes. It does not have the museum press's full timber joinery.
- Hill and crest trees are shorter and have fewer twigs than the trees by the table, so the slope stays readable. They are still the same three species.
- The tray is the cafe IKEA serving tray. It sits flat on the runner and does not dominate the close shot. The mugs and thermos are the same cafe kit, small on the table.
- Dry-stone courses are boxes with staggered lengths. Joints catch hard toon shadows. The stair where the meadow path meets the gathering terrace has a dark joint at the left wall end. The flight itself is four treads in the opening, and the path meets the bottom tread.
- There is no water, no people, and no ground-cover plants beyond tufts and a few windfalls.
