# Sources

Photographs looked at while placing the terraces, the pergola, the trees, the press, and the shed. Wikimedia Commons file pages. No reference images are stored in git.

## Used for the composition

- [Altapass Orchard](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Altapass_Orchard-27527.jpg) — Ken Thomas, public domain. Contour bands of an orchard climbing a hill, grass between the rows, depth into haze. Used for the landform. The commercial grid of small trees was not copied.
- [Dry stone walls below Shorkley Hill, Malham](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drystone_walls_below_Shorkly_Hill,_Malham_-_geograph.org.uk_-_1744014.jpg) — Peter Church, Geograph, CC BY-SA 2.0. Low irregular limestone courses stepping a slope, with an opening in the wall. Used for wall height, course size, and the stair gaps.
- [Pergola with grapevines, Lysekil](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pergola_with_grapevines.jpg) — W.carter, CC0. Light timber posts, parallel rafters, vines on the frame, open center. No diagonal braces and no solid roof.

## Used for parts

- [Apple orchard, dappled light, Boreham](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_orchard_dappled_light_at_Boreham,_Essex,_England.jpg) — Acabashi, CC BY-SA 4.0. Forked trunks and long limbs, foliage out on the limbs.
- [Apple press, Somerset Rural Life Museum](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_press.JPG) — Rodw, public domain. Heavy top beam, thin uprights, central screw, basket, trough, wicker baskets.
- [Pergola, Trentino](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pergola_trentino.JPG) — Bruce Gilardi, CC BY-SA 3.0. Round timber posts and a rustic beam.
- [Overhead grapevine arbor](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Overhead_grapevine_arbor_trellising.jpg) — Patricia Feaster, CC BY 2.0. A gnarled cane on a simple grid, sky still showing through.
- [Cotswold stone barn, Bredon Hill](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cotswold_Stone_Barn,_Bredon_Hill_-_geograph.org.uk_-_66406.jpg) — Bob Embleton, Geograph, CC BY-SA 2.0. Honey limestone courses, steep gable, clay tiles, a high vent, a narrow door. The scene shed is a one-room store with that massing, not the full barn.
