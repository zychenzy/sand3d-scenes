# Desert stargazing camp

Arid social camp at twilight. A wind-shaped sand and rock site, not a flat floor.

- Shade tent: striped canvas walls, sagging roof, poles and guy lines. Low cushions face a tea table. Rug under the seating. Open toward the path.
- Telescope deck: plank platform on a rock bench, short stair, low rail on the far sides. Fork-mounted tube with dew shield, finder, focuser and eyepiece, on a three-leg tripod with a spreader. A stool faces the eyepiece.
- Low stones and clay lamps mark a bent path between the tent and the stair.
- Plants stay sparse: ocotillo, creosote, agave, yucca, barrel cactus, cholla, sage. They are not repeated lollipops and they stay off the rug, deck and path.
- Mesas recede in layers (warm near butte, cooler center butte, right mesa, hazy far ridge).

Shots: toon default, toon corner, telescope close, unlit. Default camera holds the tent, deck and mesas. Close camera is the telescope.
