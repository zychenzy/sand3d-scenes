# SOURCES — desert stargazing camp

Composition references. These are public photos used for proportions, materials and layout. They are not copied into the repo and are not used as textures.

1. Goat-hair tents, Wadi Rum. Striped shade tents on open sand against layered sandstone.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Goat-hair_Tents_(34861965093).jpg
   David Stanley, CC BY 2.0. 29 March 2017.

2. Interior of a Bedouin tent in Wadi Rum. Dark canvas roof on poles, rugs, floor cushions along the sides, tea trays in the middle of the seating.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interior_of_a_Bedouin_tent_in_Wadi_Rum.jpg
   Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0. 11 April 2009.

3. Monument Valley at dusk. Separated buttes with talus skirts and horizontal strata, pale dusk sky, sparse gray-green scrub rather than a planted garden.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Monument_Valley_at_dusk.jpg
   John Fowler, CC BY 2.0. 13 October 2009.

4. Celestron C8 on a tripod. Optical tube, finder, rear visual back and three splayed legs meeting at a head. Used for the telescope's parts, not its branding.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Celestron_C8_Teleskop_(freigestellt).JPG
   Staycoolandbegood, public domain.

The Grand Canyon star-party photograph was looked at and not followed for lighting. It shows telescopes under a red flood and a dense star field, which hides the instruments:

Grand Canyon NPS / M. Quinn, CC BY 2.0. 25 June 2014.
