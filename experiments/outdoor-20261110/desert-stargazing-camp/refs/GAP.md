# GAP — desert stargazing camp

What the references show that this scene does not reproduce, and what the catalog did not have.

- No desert GLBs in the baked sand3d set. Terrain, tent, deck, telescope, plants, rocks and mesas are authored geometry. The only imported meshes are the cafe mug, thermos and napkin tray, scaled onto the low table.
- The tea service is those cafe props, not a brass dallah and small tea glasses. A low wooden table stands in for the cloth the trays sit on in the Wadi Rum interior.
- Captain's Desert Camp (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jordan_-_Wadi_Rum_-_Captain%27s_Desert_Camp.jpg) is an oasis with palms. Those palms were left out. Planting follows the Monument Valley scrub: few plants, different silhouettes.
- The telescope is a simplified fork mount: tube, dew shield, corrector, finder with two rings, side focuser, visual back, eyepiece, azimuth housing, two fork arms, spreader and three legs. It has no drive motors, cables, logos or setting circles.
- Cloth is a shaded mesh with a sagging roof and stripe bands, not simulated fabric. Guy lines are thin cylinders.
- Stars are 58 points in the upper sky. There is no Milky Way plate and no red flood light. Lanterns are emissive glass, not point lights, so they do not paint circular pools on the sand.
- No people, camels or vehicles.
- Mesa silhouettes are stacked strata, not scanned buttes. The far ridge is a simple hazy band.
