# Gaps

No single photograph shows this program. The scene is a corner plaza assembled from the three sources above, not a copy of any one of them.

- Paley Park is a mid-block court with a waterfall wall. The scene takes the open canopy, the dappled afternoon light on pavers, the ivy on one wall, and the clustered seats. It does not build the waterfall, the wire-chair field, or the bowl-planter rows.
- The Gansevoort view is overcast and much larger. Its foreground is a repeated row of dark planters, which this plaza does not copy. The fire escapes are also left out: their lattice would read as crossed shadows. What carries over is the brick corner, the cornice, the punched windows, the ground-floor shop edge, and the curb where a street became a plaza.
- The chess photograph is a grove of many tables. This plaza has one board, two facing chairs, and one spectator.

There is no sand3d mesh for a tree, a drinking fountain, a bicycle, a facade, a curb ramp, or a curved bench. Those are procedural. The dining chairs and tables are indoor bakes scaled to outdoor use: metal cafe chairs at the threshold, wood chairs at the board.

The integrated bench and the tactile curb ramps are part of the program. None of the three photos is a close study of either. The tactile surface is a dotted canvas on the ramp, not a truncated-dome mesh. Foliage is toon masses on branching limbs, not scanned plants. Roofs are flat caps, not a full block.
