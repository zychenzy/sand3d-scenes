# Canal-side café

A quay terrace in the Utrecht wharf pattern: olive canal in front, brick wall and café door behind, street and bicycles one storey up. Seating is three clusters (awning table, canal-view table, square table) plus a wall bench. Chairs face their table or the water. A quay path, a door-to-quay path, and the stair foot stay clear of furniture.

See `layout.json` for poses, cameras, and the checks in `test/layout.test.mjs`.
