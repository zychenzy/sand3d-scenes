# Gaps

The baked catalog has café furniture, not a quay. These pieces are procedural Toon meshes:

- Brick wall, paving, coping, and the stepped mooring
- Striped awning
- Canal surface (painted ripples, not a reflection shader)
- Hull, shelter, and ropes
- Bicycles
- Bridge arch and parapet
- Stair, street railing, lamp post
- Trees, trough, and the plants in the flowerpots

Catalog stand-ins:

- Chairs are the metal dining chair, not the woven bistro chairs in the Utrecht photos.
- Tables are a marble pedestal and a square dining table, not the black café pedestals in the photos.
- Flowerpots are the indoor kit pot. The long planter is procedural.
- No people, no commercial boat traffic, and the far bank is only a low stone lip.

The bridge is a fragment: one arch mouth and a short run of parapet, not a full crossing with a road.
