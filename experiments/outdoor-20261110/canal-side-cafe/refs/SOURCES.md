# Photo references

Used for composition, materials, and proportions. No reference images are stored in git.

## 1. Utrecht Oudegracht wharf, looking southwest

- File: [Utrecht Oudegracht aan de Werf next to Jansbrug seen from the southwest.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Utrecht_Oudegracht_aan_de_Werf_next_to_Jansbrug_seen_from_the_southwest.jpg)
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f8/Utrecht_Oudegracht_aan_de_Werf_next_to_Jansbrug_seen_from_the_southwest.jpg
- Author: Ymblanter, 12 September 2026. License: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Taken from it: water in the foreground, olive-green canal, brick coping, terrace tables along the quay, cellar wall with people at street level above, stair down to the wharf, bicycles on the upper railing, a shallow wall-mounted awning, warm afternoon light.

## 2. Same wharf, closer, from the west

- File: [Utrecht Oudegracht aan de Werf next to Jansbrug seen from the west.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Utrecht_Oudegracht_aan_de_Werf_next_to_Jansbrug_seen_from_the_west.jpg)
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cc/Utrecht_Oudegracht_aan_de_Werf_next_to_Jansbrug_seen_from_the_west.jpg
- Author: Ymblanter, 12 September 2026. License: CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Taken from it: running-bond brick, arched cellar openings, stone sills, moss at the waterline, chairs pulled up to small tables with a clear strip between the seats and the coping.

## 3. Moored boats, Bruges

- File: [Two white boats moored in a canal in Bruges, by Linh Nguyen.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Two_white_boats_moored_in_a_canal_in_Bruges,_by_Linh_Nguyen.jpg)
- Direct: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/98/Two_white_boats_moored_in_a_canal_in_Bruges%2C_by_Linh_Nguyen.jpg
- Author: Linh Nguyen, 15 February 2014. License: CC0.
- Taken from it: a small light-hulled launch with a low shelter, mooring lines to the quay, planters on the bank, and a rippled olive canal rather than a flat blue sheet.

The bridge fragment follows the brick arch bridges on this same reach (Bakkersbrug / Jansbrug), which sit at the end of the wharf view in the Oudegracht category. It is not a copy of one measured elevation.
