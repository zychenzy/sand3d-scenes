# Sources — mountain overlook

Public references used for composition, proportions, and materials. No reference images are stored in git.

## Cantilevered timber lookout

Stegastein, Aurlandsfjellet national tourist route, Norway. Todd Saunders and Tommie Wilhelmsen, 2006. A laminated-pine and steel deck, about 4 m wide and 30 m long, with a clear pane at the outer end. It leaves the cliff among pines and looks down a narrow fjord walled by layered mountains. A separate low service building sits beside the deck rather than on it.

- https://www.nasjonaleturistveger.no/en/routes/aurlandsfjellet/stegastein/
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stegastein_viewpoint_-_panoramio.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:P1000461Stegastein.JPG

The scene keeps the long narrow timber deck, the end pane, the visible beam depth, and the idea that the deck is the focal object. It is shortened to about 8.6 m so the switchback and shelter still read in one frame. See `refs/GAP.md`.

## Stone switchback on a rock shoulder

Moro Rock stairway, Sequoia National Park, California. A historic stone-and-concrete stair (about 350 steps) switchbacks up a granite dome, with a thin pipe rail on the exposed side. The route is the subject, not a path drawn on a flat pad.

- https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/moro-rock.htm
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stairs_up_to_Moro_Rock_-_sequoia_National_Park_(32020449642).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Narrow_stairway_at_the_Moro_Rock_trail_in_sequoia_national_park_(27136952663).jpg

Used for the stair rhythm, the landings at the turns, the rock the trail bends around, and the thin rail on the exposed edge. The treads here are dry-laid stone on a cut hillside, not the Moro concrete stair.

## Modest trail shelter

Gapahuk at Skjerdingvatnet, Fiksdal, Vestnes, Møre og Romsdal, Norway. A small three-walled timber hiking shelter: open front, plank walls, simple roof, set beside the trail rather than on it.

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gapahuk_shelter_by_Skjerdingvatnet_-_Fiksdal,_Vestnes,_M%C3%B8re_og_Romsdal,_Norway_-_2021-08-06.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Lean-tos_in_Norway

Used for the open-front plan, the low back wall, the plank construction, and the fire ring just outside the opening. The roof here is a turf cap, which is common on Norwegian trail shelters and is not measured from this one photograph.

## Alpine grasses, irregular pines, layered peaks

Hurricane Ridge, Olympic National Park, Washington. Subalpine meadow, scattered conifers (not a plantation grid), and successive mountain ridges. This file is a clear daytime meadow, not a sunrise. It is the reference for vegetation massing and for how near ridges sit in front of paler ranges.

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alpine_meadows_mountains_scenic_hurricane_ridge_c_bubar_august_03_2014_(17301007972).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Ridge

Sunrise color is an authored lighting scheme for this brief (warm low key from the east, cool fill, crisp air). It is not taken from a single sunrise photograph.
