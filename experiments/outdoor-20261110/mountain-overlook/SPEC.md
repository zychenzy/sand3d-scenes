# Mountain overlook — brief

A hiking destination at sunrise, not a flat platform with cone trees.

- Cantilevered timber lookout deck, glass at the outer end, stone abutment.
- Stone trail switchback with landings, cut into the shoulder.
- Topographic rock outcrop the trail turns around, with a small cairn.
- Modest three-wall trail shelter off the path, bench inside facing out, fire ring outside.
- View bench on the deck facing layered peaks, with room to walk either side.
- Alpine grass clumps, heather, and irregular pines (including krummholz and one snag).
- Crisp sunrise: warm low key from the east, cool fill, clear sky, peaks stepped back and lighter with distance.

Cameras: default fits the whole site, corner looks back from the drop at the cantilever, close stands on the deck behind the bench. See `layout.json` and `test/orientation.test.mjs`.
