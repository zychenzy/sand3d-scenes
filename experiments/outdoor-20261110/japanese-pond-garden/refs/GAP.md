# Gaps

The indoor Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse bakes in this repo are furniture: a steel-legged museum bench, a plastic flowerpot, lamps, tables. None of them read as a timber bridge, a stone lantern, a maple, or a pond edge, so this scene does not instance them. Terrain, water, bridge, lantern, bench, and plants are authored geometry.

Honest limits against the photographs:

- The Wrocław bridge is mirrored in the water. This toon setup has no reflection pass. Depth comes from a darker basin, transparent green water, submerged pebbles, lilies, and koi.
- Koi are simplified bodies (white, orange, black patches), not individual scale patterns.
- Kiyosumi's distant building is a full pavilion. The brief asks for a shaded tea bench, so the seat is an open timber shade with a slat roof, not that building.
- Ritsurin's bridge is a long span on several thin piles. This span is the shorter Wrocław garden bridge, so it has one center pile and stone abutments.
- Foliage is geometric: lobed maple cards, cone tufts for the pines, hanging willow strands, reed blades. Not scanned plants.
- Moss is a set of ground patches on the banks and under the trees, not a continuous moss temple.
- The background hill fades in morning fog. It is not a photographic mountain plate.
- The stone lantern is a low pond-side yukimi, about 1.1 m, so it stays secondary to the bridge.
