# Seaside promenade

A short bay promenade you can walk from end to end.

- Stone wall follows a curve: inland in the middle, hooking seaward on the left. Parapet is low enough to see the water.
- Tiered steps in the bay drop from the walk to the sand, clear of the through path.
- A timber deck projects over the right-hand beach, with a rail on the water sides and a bench facing out.
- Four catalog benches sit along the wall or on the deck, long axis with the shore.
- Gelato kiosk and two planters are landward of the walk. A three-hoop bicycle stand sits by the entrance, off the path.
- Dune clumps and beach grasses are separate silhouettes. Rocks gather at the left waterline. Three sails sit offshore.

Cameras, colours, and the acceptance list are in `layout.json`.
