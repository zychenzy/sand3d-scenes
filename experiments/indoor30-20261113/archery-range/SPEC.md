# Gold Line — 18 m indoor

Metres, Y up. Archers look toward −Z. `layout.json` places the butts, quivers, bows, and waiting furniture. `range_spec.mjs` is the alignment contract the tests enforce.

## Field

World Archery indoor distance is 18 m from a point under the gold to the shooting line, tolerance ±10 cm. Here the white face of every butt is exactly 18.00 m from the target-side edge of the shooting line. Gold rings stand about 30 mm proud of that face, inside the tolerance. Faces are vertical (the indoor butt angle is 0–10°). Each butt carries a vertical 40 cm three-spot. The middle centre is 1.30 m. Centres are 0.42 m apart.

Four lanes on 1.80 m centres (the indoor minimum is 0.80 m, previously 0.70 m). The arrow corridor is the central 0.80 m of each lane from 1.55 m (past arrow relief) to the shooting line. Nothing stands in it.

## Lines

- 3 m line: target-side edge 3.00 m in front of the shooting line. The 50 mm width lies inside that 3 m.
- Shooting line: 50 mm, width on the athlete side of the 18 m edge.
- Waiting line: target-side edge 3.00 m behind the shooting line.
- Equipment line: 4.00 m behind the waiting line.

Ground quivers sit on the lane shoulder, behind the shooting line and in front of the waiting line, outside the corridor. Bow rack, hip quivers, and preparation benches are behind the waiting line. Spectator benches and the scoring table are behind the equipment line. Two clear paths: a cross aisle just behind the waiting line, and an entrance along the open side.

## Light

Hard four-step toon. The key sits under the ceiling so the lid does not throw a room-sized shadow. Ceiling, net, and battens do not cast. Battens are emissive, so they stay lit when the view switches to unlit. No vertical sign is placed in the camera cone; lane numbers lie on the floor.
