# GAP — Gold Line

Candidate renders were inspected from `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse`. Reference photographs were not committed.

## Used

- DanielMcGavin2898 recurve. Clean 1.70 m stick bow. The catalog assembly includes a nocked arrow; the rack export drops it. Four limb colors are re-exports, not new models. The string stays on the exported tip plane.
- Iben_Vroman hip quiver. Leather tube, strap, four shafts. Fletching is a drab olive, so it does not read as bright club vanes. Kept anyway because the body is a real quiver.

## Rejected after looking at the renders

- ragnar “Target (archery)” (`bafac3d3d41048fa`). Round outdoor boss, no scoring rings, stand tilted far past the indoor 0–10° limit, arrows stuck in the back. Not a face you can shoot.
- tuturu belt quivers. Crossbow bolts, near-black, fantasy plates.
- AliNagi “High Poly Bow and Arrow”. A grey stick with the arrow crossing the limb.
- ttfh3500 “AH-64D Apache Longbow”. A helicopter.
- pacoalgebra2 “Arco” (`f4a6f0a1ef354053ba614afc52868aa5`). Program kept at `programs/grip_bow__f4a6f0a1ef35.py`. The nocked arrow was dropped and the mesh was baked, but the mirrored upper limb landed on top of the lower limb and the string ran past both tips to the floor. It is not in the scene.

## Authored because the catalog did not cover them

- Indoor block butt with a vertical 40 cm three-spot. Middle centre 1.30 m, centres 0.42 m apart. Layered foam, steel stand. Gold is about 30 mm proud of the white face; the 18.00 m distance is taken to the white face.
- Ground quiver: tripod, cup, six fletched arrows. This is the bright vane color the hip quiver lacks.
- Backstop net, skirt, lane runners, lines, bow rack, preparation benches, stuck arrows, floor numbers. An overhead ribbon baffle was tried at the shooting line and removed: it read as a cage under the ceiling.

## Still approximate

- The net is a ribbon curtain, not woven mesh.
- Arm guard, tab, bow square, and arrow tubes on the benches are simple solids.
- The flowerpot bake is an empty pot. A two-sphere shrub sits in it.
- The chalkboard is a classroom board on the side wall, not a printed results sheet. It faces the side aisle so it is not a billboard in the downrange view.
- No archers, no bowstands on the line, no ceiling-hung butt.
- Hip-quiver straps are large relative to the tube. They hang in front of the rack, clear of the aisle.
- One hard key under the ceiling. The lid, net, and battens do not cast, so the maple does not go black and the lines do not shimmer.
