Asset programs: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse (CC-BY-4.0). Per-mesh credits are in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.

The local bake set has no camera, tripod, spotlight, or puppet. Those gallery names exist on the dataset and were not re-baked here. The rig, flags, puppets, and armatures are procedural stand-ins.
