# Stop-motion studio — brief

A working animation stage. The miniature street is the subject. The room around it is human scale.

- Raised table (top at 1.00 m) with a dropped stage, three houses no taller than 0.46 m, and an 0.18 m puppet standing in the street.
- Motion-control column and head aimed at the puppet, with a micro fresnel, a flag, and a bounce card on the tabletop. A pencil (0.18 m) and a jam jar are the scale cues.
- Fabrication bench beside the stage: cutting mat, clay heads, jars, a banker's lamp. The chair faces the bench.
- Frame desk on the left wall: monitor glass toward the chair, frame-grab, dope sheet, lamp, mug.
- Armature rack against the back-left wall, storyboard on the easel, walk-cycle cards, a review puppet on a plinth.
- Sage wainscot, timber floor, a blackout window, and a side door. No cyclorama, no softbox grid, no dress form.

Shading is offline: four-step MeshToon (darkest stop 0.32) with a hard shadow map, and a true MeshBasic unlit path. Measurable poses are in `layout.json`.
