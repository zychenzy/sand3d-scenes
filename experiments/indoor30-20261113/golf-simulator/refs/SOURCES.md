Bay size used here, checked against those rooms rather than a minimum closet: about 4.2 m of screen, 3.8 m from ball to screen, 3.7 m ceiling, and a 1.58 m swing radius that stays inside each net. Asset programs: Hugging Face `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse`, CC-BY-4.0. See `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
