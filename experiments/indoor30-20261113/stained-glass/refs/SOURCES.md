# Sources

Links only. No reference photos are stored in this folder.

The room is a cold shop: pattern and light table, sheet racks, copper foil, solder with ventilation, and a finished leaded panel. It is not a hot shop.

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stained_glass — cut sheet, lead came, copper foil as the modern alternative, lead-handling caution
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Came_glasswork — came versus copper foil, soldered joints, foil for small intricate pieces
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_lamp — cartoon, numbered cut pieces, copper-foil wrap, solder on a bench
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Comfort_Tiffany — copper-foil patent used for windows and lamps, distinct from blown Favrile
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Stained_glass — finished panels and glass colour, not a workshop plan
- https://www.cavemanglass.com/post/from-classroom-to-craft-room-how-to-set-up-your-own-stained-glass-workspace — separate pattern/cut, solder-with-ventilation, and storage zones, plus a clear shared bench
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ViY-1FMCEf4 — MakeHaven cold shop: light boards, foil, grinder, iron, running pliers, grozers, cutter
