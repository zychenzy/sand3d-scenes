# Salle Montjoie

Metres, Y up. The far wall is the more negative Z. The near end is a section cut, not a fourth wall. `layout.json` holds cameras, lights, and the furniture that must stay off the strips.

## Pistes

FIE technical rules t.18 / t.19, as in the August 2026 edition. Each piste is 14 m by 1.50 m. On-guard lines sit 2 m either side of centre. The centre line is broken across the width. The last 2 m before each rear limit is a contrasting warning colour. Rear-limit lines are 50 mm and sit on the 14 m, not beyond it. Conductive borders are omitted: t.18.3 says they are not part of the piste.

Piste A is centred at x = −2.20, piste B at x = +2.20. Inner edges are 2.90 m apart. That aisle is a club choice so a referee and a passer do not share a shoulder. It is not a codified FIE minimum. Runbacks are 2.00 m of floor beyond each rear limit, inside the piste’s x range only. Score machines stand about 1.45 m off the outer edge, inside the 1–5 m range in t.18.4, and face the aisle. A second (slave) machine is not fitted. The rules put that one at least 5 m past the runback.

## Room

Walled length runs from z = −9.15 to the cut at z = 8.75. Clear height is 5.08. Side walls are plaster above a 1.18 m green wainscot, with a chair rail, baseboard, and a clerestory. The floor is oak planks 85 mm wide, running the length of the hall. Piste bodies are a cool aluminum with a low-contrast panel shift and a seam every 2 m, warning ends a dull rose, lines cream. The oak around them stays warm and narrower. The far door is centred on the referee aisle, not on a piste.

The left wall is the armory: a row of masks on a peg rail, one mask hung immediately beside the weapon rack, lamés, a glove shelf, then foils, epees, and sabres bell-down so the lip meets the rail. The right bay is the coach’s place under the soffit: a plain writing table, a backless stool on the wall side of the table looking across it toward the pistes, a notebook, and a wall-mounted distance diagram (en garde, straight attack, parry 4). A coach’s box is marked on the floor beside piste B. Four ceiling reels sit outside the piste edges. Spectator benches sit against the side walls at the cut and face down the hall. A side bench further along the right wall faces piste B.

## Light

Hard toon, `NoToneMapping`, `PCFHardShadowMap`. The gradient’s darkest step is 104/255 so lit surfaces do not crush to black. Ceiling, beams, soffit, and battens do not cast. Blades, the rack, and the masks do not cast either: the mesh and the thin steel were stippling the plaster. Contact under the furniture is a blob, not a second shadow.
