# SOURCES — Salle Montjoie

Piste length, width, on-guard lines, warning colour, rear limits, and score-machine distance follow the FIE technical rules, t.18 and t.19, August 2026 edition:

https://static.fie.org/uploads/40/204126-Technical%20rules%20August%202026%20ang.pdf

https://static.fie.org/uploads/40/204138-Technical%20rules%20August%202026%20ang.pdf

https://fie.org/documents/rules

Field of play summary used as a cross-check:

https://dev-fie-api.britishfencing.com/technical_sections/field-play/

https://fencing.thegearsports.com/learn/the-piste/

The 2.90 m aisle is a club clearance, not a rule. Strips set too close are a known way for a hit to land on the next bout. That note is from the West Coast Fencing Archive:

https://westcoastfencingarchive.com/2019/09/30/the-salle-in-the-woods/

Equipment silhouettes (mask bib, bell guards, French grip, scoring lamps) were checked against the Wikimedia Commons fencing-equipment category. No photograph is stored in this scene:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Fencing_equipment

The Objaverse catalog was searched for mask, foil, epee, sabre, scoring box, and weapon rack. None of those are in `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse`:

https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse
