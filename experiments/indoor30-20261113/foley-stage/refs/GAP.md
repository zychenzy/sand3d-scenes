# GAP — foley stage

The local catalog has desks, chairs, a monitor, a speaker, a table, lamps, a tulip stool, a bench, and tabletop props. It has no shotgun microphone, character shoe, stage door, footstep pit, or splash basin. Those meshes are baked in this folder. The prop seat is a plain four-leg work stool built in the scene, not the cafe tulip. Everything else is referenced from `cowork/`, `music/`, `workshop/`, `cafe/`, and `museum/` and is not copied.

What the photos do not settle:

- The Wikimedia foley-room photo shows one worked floor and a mic, not three separated pits plus a door rig and a glazed booth. The 14 m stage, the metre aisles, and the booth placement are composed from the practice on the Wikipedia page, not traced from one picture.
- No photo in the set shows a modern suspended shotgun over a labeled pit. The bake is a 28 cm tube with a slotted barrel, a shock mount, and a grille at local +Z. Pit and water mics point down at the surface (layout test and the runtime probe). The close frame is a side view of that barrel, the boom, and the cable over the wood pit. It is still a simple mesh, not a scanned microphone.
- The door is the same hinged slab on casters, ajar, with a small latch. It is not a scanned stage door, and there is no separate sliding panel.
- The basin stays where it was, off the walkways. The rim is an open frame and the water surface is a flat volume, so the default view can see into the tub. From the corner it is still a small object.
- Shoes are blocky oxfords, 48 on the racks plus a pair on a crate. They are not scanned footwear.
- Gravel and sand are 128² canvases plus a few pebbles and two footprint dents. Wood is ten colored planks. None of that is a scan.
- The cloth rail, thunder sheet, stair unit, baffles, pipe grid, and booth shell are boxes and cylinders.
- The cue booth has a small sync strip and one catalog speaker inside the glass. That is the engineer's desk. It is not a recording-studio console, and it is still a simplification of a dub stage.
- There is no walker. Pathways are clearance lines around the pits, the basin, and the prop keep-outs.
- A ceiling plane sits over the pipe grid and the clouds. The +X and +Z sides stay open. Cameras stand inside the floor footprint so the cut edge is not the subject. This is still a cutaway, not a sealed room.
