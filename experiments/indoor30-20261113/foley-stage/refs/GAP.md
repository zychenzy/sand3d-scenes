# GAP — foley stage

The local catalog has desks, chairs, a monitor, a speaker, a table, lamps, a stool, a bench, and tabletop props. It has no shotgun microphone, character shoe, rolling stage door, footstep pit, or splash basin. Those five meshes are baked in this folder. Everything else is referenced from `cowork/`, `music/`, `workshop/`, `cafe/`, and `museum/` and is not copied.

What the photos do not settle:

- The Wikimedia foley-room photo shows one worked floor and a mic, not three separated pits plus a door rig and a glazed booth. The 14 m stage, the metre aisles, and the booth placement are composed from the practice on the Wikipedia page, not traced from one picture.
- No photo in the set shows a modern suspended shotgun over a labeled pit. Capsules are plain tubes. Local +Z is the grille, and the pit and water mics point down at the surface (layout test and the runtime probe). In the close shot the hang is nearly vertical, so the lean toward the middle of the wood pit is easy to miss.
- The door is a paneled slab on casters, ajar, with a small latch. It reads as a door from the default camera. It is not a scanned stage door, and the latch is small at wide framing.
- The basin is a metal tub with a flat water volume. From the corner camera you mostly see the side of the tub, so the water surface is easy to miss.
- Shoes are blocky oxfords, 48 on the racks plus a pair on a crate. They are not scanned footwear.
- Gravel and sand are 128² canvases plus a few pebbles and two footprint dents. Wood is ten colored planks. None of that is a scan.
- The cloth rail, thunder sheet, stair unit, baffles, pipe grid, and booth shell are boxes and cylinders.
- The cue booth has a small sync strip and one catalog speaker inside the glass. That is the engineer's desk. It is not a recording-studio console, and it is still a simplification of a dub stage.
- There is no walker. Pathways are clearance lines around the pits, the basin, and the prop keep-outs.
- The +X and +Z sides are open so the camera can see the floor. This is a cutaway, not a sealed room.
