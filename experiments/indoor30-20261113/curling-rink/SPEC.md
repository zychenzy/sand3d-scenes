# Sheet 2

Metres, Y up. The sheet centre is the origin. +Z is the home end, the end the default camera looks from.

## Sheet

World Curling Federation R1, July 2024, at the maximum club size. Length 45.720 from inside back board to inside back board. Width 4.750 from inside side line to inside side line. Tee lines sit 17.375 from the centre. The back line’s outside edge is 1.829 from the tee. The hog line is 102 mm wide and its inside edge is 6.401 from the tee. The centre line runs between the hack lines, 3.658 past each tee. Each house is the 12 ft / 8 ft / 4 ft / button set: outer radii 1.829, 1.219, 0.610, 0.152. The 12-foot band is blue, the 4-foot is red, the button is white.

Two hacks at each end. The inside edge of each is 76 mm off the centre line, the block is 152 mm wide, and it extends 190 mm in front of the hack line (the rule allows 203 mm). They stand 40 mm proud so the rubber reads in toon; a recessed hack would disappear.

Stones are 286 mm across (circumference under the 914 mm limit) and 120 mm tall, above the 114 mm minimum. Handles are red or yellow and numbered. One set of sixteen: six red and seven yellow still in the home racks, two red and one yellow in play (one in the 4-foot, one in the 8-foot, one guard short of the house). The far racks are empty timber, the other end of the same set, not a second sixteen.

## Building

The ice hall is narrow: walks outside the side lines, back boards, a roof at 5.04, and trusses on a 4.4 m rhythm. The home −X wall and the home end above the back board are cut away. The cut face is dark poche. The far end keeps a wall and a clerestory so the length closes instead of running into a void.

The lounge is only at the home end, on +X. Its floor is 160 mm above the apron, oak, under a soffit at 3.22. Glass and a 1.05 m doorway separate it from the ice. Benches face the sheet. A sofa, round table, chair, mugs, and two plants sit off the 0.80 m aisle along the glass. The long coffee-table bake was measured and rejected: at a usable height it is about 2.5 m long and would close that aisle.

The near scoreboard is 1.36 × 0.42, hung over the side of the home end, not over the house. The far board is the same size on the end wall. A 160 mm plate on the glass is the only other text.

## Light

Hard 4-step toon, hard shadows, no bloom. The key models the lounge and the home end. The ice plane does not receive that shadow, so a 46 m floor does not break into acne; contact blobs sit under racks, stones, and furniture. Cool linear fixtures run under the trusses. Warm point lights sit in the lounge. Unlit mode replaces materials with `MeshBasicMaterial` and keeps the fixture colour by mixing in the emissive.
