Shared furniture is symlinked from existing scene bakes of Hugging Face `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse` programs (CC-BY). Those files are not copied. The padlock is the one new bake, exported from the dataset program in `programs/padlock__c9f0cf0cbbe6.py`. The pegwall, duplicator, vises, cylinders, mortise locks, rekey bench, and handoff counter are original geometry in `locksmith.js`.
