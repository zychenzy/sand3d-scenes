Layout notes used as a checklist, not as a photo: a grooming floor keeps the tub in a wet zone, the tables in a dry zone, kennels within a short walk, and a handoff at reception. Public pages: [GroomLio salon layout](https://groomlio.app/en/blog/grooming-salon-design-layout) and [PAWS motion-economy examples](https://pawsdogdaycare.com/Start-Up-Services/dog-grooming/economy-motion-design-examples).
