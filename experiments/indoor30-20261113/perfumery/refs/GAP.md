# Gaps

Inspected catalog GLBs (bounds, materials, node facing) before placing any of them. Nothing in the local bakes is a flacon, a beam balance, a pipette, a blotter, or a scent organ.

- Flacons, the two-pan balance, and the pipette are original low-poly bakes (`bake_assets.py`). Glass is an opaque toon color. There is no refraction. Caps, collars, and pumps carry the silhouette.
- The organ, hood, louver, window, drawers, blotters, and mixing glasses are procedural. The arc is eight straight bays, so the curve is faceted.
- Jam jars are the cafe concentrate bake (dark glass, gold lid). Two sit on the material chest. They are not used as the organ bottles.
- Display bookcases shipped without materials. They are tinted walnut and filled with formula volumes.
- The museum plinth shipped without materials. It is tinted stone and kept under a metre so it stays a pedestal. One larger flacon stands on it.
- The museum vitrine bake is a solid walnut shell, not glass. It is not in the room.
- The wall-shelf bake was tried on the back wall and removed. Its boards fought the wall and clipped the jars. Concentrates sit on the drawer top instead.
- The book-stand bake is offset by about 5.4 m and reads as a dark podium. The formula is a flat card on the bench (`No. 14`).
- Bar counter, bar stool, mug, espresso machine, school desk, and chalkboard were not used. Those read as the cafe or the science lab.
- The flowerpot bake is an empty pot. A small citrus plant is built on top of it.
- Contact shadows under the chairs and bench are discs. Shelf bays, the organ flacons, the floor slab, and the hood canopy do not cast. An arc of boards and the hood's shadow left grey stains on the open boards. Chairs, the bench, the table, and the bookcases still cast. The ceiling is one plaster soffit with a wood fascia and two beams, so the cutaway does not open onto sky. Floorboards are two close tones so the stripe stays quiet in unlit.
- The extract hood is millwork, not a scanned fume cupboard. See the rejected hood link in `SOURCES.md`.
- Drawer labels are legible up close and small in the wide shot. Family plates on the organ read from the corner camera.
