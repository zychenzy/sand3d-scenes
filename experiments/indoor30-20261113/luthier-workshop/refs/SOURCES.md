# Sources

Links only. Reference photos are not in git.

Workshop layout (bench, hanging instruments, varnish, handoff) follows public luthier shops:

- [Luthier's workshops](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Luthier%27s_workshops) — category of shop interiors.
- [Atelier de lutherie à Mirecourt](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atelier_de_lutherie_%C3%A0_Mirecourt.jpg) — bench, tools, and instruments in one room.
- [Atelier d'un luthier, Mirecourt, 2013](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atelier_dun_luthier_%281%29,_Mirecourt,_2013.jpg) — workbench and hanging tools.
- [In the workshop of a luthier](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:In_the_workshop_of_a_luthier_05.jpg) — repair bench at working distance.
- [Instrument repair workshop, Leipzig](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Instrument_repair_workshop,_MfM.Uni-Leipzig.jpg) — repair room rather than a practice studio.
- [Applying varnish on an unfinished violin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Applying_Varnish_on_Unfinished_Violin.jpg) — varnish work.
- [Varnishing a violin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Varnishing_a_violin.jpg) — varnish nook.
- [Bending iron](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biegeeisen.jpg) — rib bending iron.
- [Violin construction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Violin_construction) — molds, plates, and f-holes.
- [Artisan's tools of the trade](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Artisan%27s_Tools_of_the_Trade.jpg) — guitar bench tools, Matt Rubendall, 2018.

Wikimedia pages carry the license for each file. This scene does not copy those photographs.

Shared GLBs are existing bakes of Hugging Face [Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse) (CC-BY). Credits are in `../ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
