# Field Opticians

One cutaway, two rooms.

## Retail

Oak floor, plaster, a shop window on the left wall. Three felt cabinets on the back wall, two glass trays in the aisle, reception at the front-left, a waiting bench facing the shop. The fitting bay is the left-wall mirror: bulbs, a ledge of frames, a chair facing the glass, and a school desk used as the adjustment bench.

## Exam

East of the partition and behind the door. Linoleum, a lower ceiling, a chart on the back wall, the patient chair facing that chart, a phoropter between them, a trial cabinet of lens discs, a writing desk, and a stool for the practitioner.

## Facing

Dining-chair front is +Z at rotation 0.

- Fitting chair and adjustment chair use −π/2 and face −X, toward the mirror and the bench.
- Staff faces −Z across the desk. The consult chair faces +Z toward staff.
- The patient chair is built facing −Z, toward the chart. The phoropter is between the chair and the chart.
- The trial case opens +X into the lane (rotation −π/2; the case front is local −Z).

## Light

Hard stepped toon by default. One warm key from the open front, a cool window fill, and small pools at the pendants, the bench lamp, the chart, and the exam ceiling. Bulbs stay basic-white so they read in both modes. Unlit ignores lights and uses basic materials. No bloom, no outline, no tone map.
