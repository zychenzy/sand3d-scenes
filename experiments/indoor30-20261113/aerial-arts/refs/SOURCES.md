# SOURCES — Span Room

No reference photos are stored in git. Links only.

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerial_silk — two fabric tails, figure-eight or ring, swivel, screw-gate carabiner, spanset around a beam. Fabric that reaches the floor is the usual training setup.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerial_hoop — lyra is a circular steel apparatus on a spanset, single tab or double tab.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_rigging — aerial rigging is a dead hang from rated structure, not a scenery batten.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat_(gymnastics) — crash mats are the thick landing pads; sting mats are the thin ones this room does not use.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:AerialShowgirlsTissu1.jpg — typology photo of tissu, hoop, and static trapeze together. Not checked in.

Scale used here is a design assumption, not a quoted code clause. The bay is 7.25 m to the slab. Each lyra is 1.0 m across. Each mat is 2.10 × 2.70 × 0.34 m, centered on the hang point. The waiting bench is east of the mats and south of the floor line.
