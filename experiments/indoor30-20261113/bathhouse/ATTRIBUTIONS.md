# Attributions

GLBs are symlinks. Nothing here recopies those files. Three.js is the build already in `gym/vendor/` (symlink).

- **Cash register** `cash_register__870f6ebb115a.glb` → `cafe/assets/` — Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse, CC-BY-4.0, existing cafe bake. Used on the bandai.
- **Wall shelf** `wall_shelf__8732f5e14a30.glb` → `cafe/assets/` — "CC0 - Wall Shelf 4" by plaggy, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/8732f5e14a30461f83bcb34a9101cbc0, CC-BY-4.0. Existing cafe bake. Used as the towel shelf. Near-black paints on the register are lifted in the scene so the toon bands do not crush.

Lockers, benches, shoe cubbies, stools, buckets, taps, screens, noren, tile, and the tub rim are procedural meshes in `bathhouse.js`. No new external models.
