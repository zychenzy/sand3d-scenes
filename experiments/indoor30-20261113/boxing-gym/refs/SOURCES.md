Planning rules, used as design assumptions rather than a copied specification:

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxing_ring — square raised platform, four ropes on corner posts, red corner and blue corner. The page describes a standard ring as 16–24 ft (4.9–7.3 m) inside the ropes, about 2 ft (0.61 m) of apron, a platform 3–4 ft (0.91–1.22 m) high, rope heights of 18, 30, 42, and 54 in (0.46, 0.76, 1.07, 1.37 m), and posts about 5 ft (1.5 m) above the canvas.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punching_bag
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_bag

This scene uses the small end of that range: 4.88 m inside the ropes, a 0.52 m apron, deck at 0.98 m, those four rope heights, and posts 1.52 m above the canvas. Heavy-bag centres are 2.6 m apart so a person can stand between the stands. The strike lane in front of each bag is 1.2 m, and the walk from the end of that lane to the apron is kept above 0.9 m.
