# SOURCES — Nettleton table tennis club

No reference photos are stored in git. Links only.

- Table, net, and ball: [ITTF Statutes / Handbook](https://www.ittf.com/statutes/), Laws of Table Tennis 2.1–2.3. Table 2.74 m × 1.525 m × 0.76 m. White edge line 2 cm. Centre line 3 mm, parallel to the side lines. Net and posts 15.25 cm, each post 15.25 cm outside the side line. Ball 40 mm.
- Club bay, not a title court: [Manchester Table Tennis Club, Sunday training hall](https://buymeacoffee.com/manttc/sunday-training-hall-table-layout). Three tables, wood floor, each court boxed to 10 m × 5 m with barriers between courts.
- Hall with several tables under a long roof: [Table tennis in the 1976 Hall, Kirkgate Market, Leeds](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Table_tennis_in_the_1976_Hall,_Kirkgate_Market,_Leeds.jpg) (Andy Li, CC0).
- Social club interior (tables, pendant light, side seating): [SPiN San Francisco](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SPiN_San_Francisco_2.jpg) (Jeff Terrell, CC BY 2.0).
- Meshes: Hugging Face `Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse`, CC-BY-4.0. Credits are in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
