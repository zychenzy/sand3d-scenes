# SOURCES — civic maple roller rink

No reference photos are stored in git. These are the pictures the plan was checked against:

- Crystal Palace Skating Rink, Las Vegas. Maple floor, perimeter barrier, globe lights on a ceiling rig, mirror ball, and a DJ position at the far side. The photo is much more neon than this room; the globe rhythm and the maple oval were kept, the mural and the colored wash were not. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crystal_Palace_Skating_Rink_Las_Vegas,_Nevada.jpg
- Franklin Skate Club, exterior. Barrel-roof civic rink, used for the vault rather than a flat lid. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Franklin_Skate_Club_-_May_2022_-_Sarah_Stierch.jpg
- Oaks Park painted check-in sign. Used as a warning: a mural or a big wordmark in the main view reads as a billboard, so this room has no sign facing the camera. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oaks_Park_Roller_Skating_Rink,_2018_-_4.jpg
- Roller rink, overview of the oval and the rail: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_rink
- Asset programs and existing bakes: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Sandflow/sand3d-objaverse
